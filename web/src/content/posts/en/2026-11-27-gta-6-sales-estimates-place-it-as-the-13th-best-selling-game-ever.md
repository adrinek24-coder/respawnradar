GTA 6 has reached an estimated 53 million copies sold and $4.7 billion in gross revenue after just one week on the market, according to market analysis shared on November 27, 2026 by Alinea Analytics' Rhys Elliott. If accurate, that puts Rockstar's November 19 release at #13 on the all-time best-selling video games list—jumping past Animal Crossing: New Horizons (50.78M), Pokémon Red/Blue/Yellow combined (47.44M), Cyberpunk 2077, The Sims, and every standalone Call of Duty game. Keep in mind that 53 million is a third-party analyst estimate; Rockstar and Take-Two have only officially confirmed the launch-day numbers so far.

## Official Launch Numbers vs. Week-One Estimates

- **Week-one estimate (Alinea Analytics):** 53 million copies sold and $4.7 billion in revenue across both consoles as of November 27, 2026.
- **PS5 vs. Xbox Series X|S split (estimated):** PS5 dominates with 75.7% of copies (around 40 million units and 76.1% of revenue), while Xbox Series X|S accounts for 24.3% (around 13 million units). US buyers make up 33.5% of PS5 copies.
- **Rockstar / Take-Two official figures:** 32.5 million units sold before release and $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales—the biggest entertainment launch in history and nearly five times GTA V's ~$800M first 24 hours.
- **Sony official PS5 stats:** Over 39 million unique players booted up GTA 6 on PS5 between November 19 and 22, logging 683 million cumulative hours (17.4 hours per player) and setting a PlayStation record for concurrent players.
- **Player grind stats (estimated):** Around 7 million players have already crossed 50 hours played, and 250,000 have topped 100 hours inside the first week.
- **Review score:** Sitting at a 99 Metascore on Metacritic.

## Pro Tips & What's Easy to Mix Up

- **Don't equate Sony's 39M players with 39M sales:** Sony's PlayStation Blog number tracks unique accounts, not units sold—game sharing and multiple household accounts on one console inflate player counts compared to copies bought. Wait for Take-Two's next earnings report for audited total unit sales.
- **Clear 212 GB on your fast SSD before buying:** GTA 6 eats up to 212 GB of storage. External USB hard drives won't launch native PS5 or Series X|S builds, so clear internal SSD space or grab a compatible NVMe drive/expansion card first.
- **Skip fake PC keys and downloaders:** Rockstar has not announced a PC version yet. Any site listing cheap GTA 6 PC keys or cracked installers right now is a scam.
- **No multiplayer launch date yet:** Only the single-player campaign is live right now; Rockstar hasn't dated the multiplayer expansion. If you're debating Standard ($79.99) vs. Ultimate ($99.99), there's no immediate Online rush forcing you into the pricier tier.
