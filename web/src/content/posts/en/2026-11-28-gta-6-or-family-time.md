You do not have to fight over the living room TV or skip family time to keep grinding Leonida: both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S support Remote Play for GTA 6 on phones, tablets, PCs, handhelds, and PlayStation Portal, plus Cloud Streaming is live for digital owners. Because GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 exclusively for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with no native PC or mobile port yet, Remote Play and Cloud Streaming are the fastest official ways to play away from the couch.

## How to Set Up GTA 6 on PS5 Remote Play & PlayStation Portal

If someone else grabbed the TV or you are traveling to visit relatives, you can stream GTA 6 from your own PS5 to an iPhone, Android phone, iPad, Windows PC, Mac, or PlayStation Portal — or use PS Plus Premium cloud streaming directly on Portal without even needing a PS5 powered on.

- On your PS5, open `Settings > System > Remote Play` and toggle **Enable Remote Play** on.
- So your device can wake the console remotely, head to `Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode` and turn on both **Stay Connected to the Internet** and **Enable Turning On PS5 from Network**.
- Install the official **PS Remote Play** app on your phone, tablet, PC, or Mac, sign in with the same PlayStation account, pair a DualSense controller (or use on-screen touch controls in a pinch), select your PS5, and boot up GTA 6.
- **PS Portal Cloud Streaming (no PS5 required):** You need a digital copy of GTA 6, an active **PS Plus Premium** membership, and an adult account. On your Portal home screen, open the **Cloud Streaming** tab in the upper-left corner, select **Streaming Games in Your Library**, pick **Grand Theft Auto VI**, and hit **Stream**.

## How to Set Up GTA 6 on Xbox Remote Play & Xbox Cloud Gaming

Xbox Series X|S players can stream GTA 6 for free over Remote Play to smartphones, laptops, desktops, or handheld gaming PCs like the Xbox ROG Ally. If you only have an Xbox One or want to avoid installing the 212 GB file on Series X|S, Xbox Cloud Gaming is also available on Xbox consoles.

- On your Xbox Series X|S, go to `Settings > Devices & connections > Remote features` and check **Enable remote features**.
- Click **Test remote play** right in that menu to verify your home network before leaving the room.
- **On a phone or tablet:** Open your mobile browser, visit `play.xbox.com/remoteplay`, sign in with your Microsoft account, pick your console, and choose **Remote Play on This Device**.
- **On PC or Xbox ROG Ally:** Open the **Xbox on PC** app, click your profile icon in the top-right corner, select **Accessories & remote play**, and choose **Remote play** under your console.
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming (Xbox One & Series X|S consoles only):** Press the Xbox button, search for **Grand Theft Auto VI**, select **Manage > See in Microsoft Store**, and choose **Play With Cloud**. You must own the digital Xbox Store copy and have cloud playtime (Game Pass Essential gives 5 hrs/month, Premium gives 10 hrs/month, Ultimate gives 15 hrs/month, with extra hours purchasable).

## Pro Tips & Easy-to-Miss Traps

- **Wire your console first:** Plug your PS5 or Xbox straight into your router with Ethernet and put your phone, Portal, or handheld on 5 GHz Wi-Fi. High-speed chases in Vice City turn into a blurry mess fast if both ends are fighting over congested 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
- **Leave the console in Rest/Sleep Mode before traveling:** Fully shutting down your PS5 or Xbox kills remote wake-up. Test connecting once over cellular data before heading out for the weekend.
- **Watch the PS Portal account mismatch trap:** Your PS Plus Premium subscription must be on the exact same PlayStation account that bought the digital copy of GTA 6 — game-sharing from a second account will block Portal cloud streaming.
- **Drop manual saves during short holiday sessions:** GTA 6 gives you 15 manual save slots, and completed story missions cannot be replayed from a menu at launch. Since the main campaign spans 91 missions across 5 chapters (~49–60 hours), quick handheld sessions add up — always drop a manual save before major story choices or putting the screen down.
