**Quick Answer / TL;DR:** GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 without an official full first-person mode. However, players discovered a controller remapping glitch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S that locks the camera behind Jason's or Lucia's eyes, letting you freely explore Leonida, drive, and fire weapons in first-person until you reload a save.

## How to Activate Full First-Person Mode in GTA 6

To trigger the unofficial first-person perspective, you need to rebind one controller button and perform a quick sequence inside a vehicle:

- Pause the game, open **Settings**, and navigate to the **Controller** tab.
- Scroll down to **Controller Features** and locate the **Touch Pad Button** (PS5) or **View Button** (Xbox) setting.
- Change its assignment from **Map** to **Camera** (displayed as **Camera 2 / 2**).
- Get inside any car and sit in the driver's seat.
- Press **Up on the D-pad** to pull out your in-game smartphone.
- Press **Circle** (PS5) or **B** (Xbox) to put the phone away, and **immediately spam** the **Touch Pad** (PS5) or **View Button** (Xbox).
- Once the timing lands, you will stay in a first-person cockpit view with no phone visible—and the camera stays in first-person even after you exit the car.

## Official First-Person Views vs. Full First-Person Glitch

| Mode / Perspective | PS5 Input | Xbox Series X|S Input | Free Movement | How to Exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inspect Mode (Official) | Hold L2 + press R3 (unarmed) | Hold LT + press RS (unarmed) | No, standing still only | Move your character |
| Weapon Optics (Official) | Hold L2 + press R3 (supported scope) | Hold LT + press RS (supported scope) | While aiming only | Release aim |
| Phone in Car (Official) | Press D-pad Up while driving | Press D-pad Up while driving | Driving only | Press Circle / B to close phone |
| Full First-Person Glitch (Unofficial) | Rebind Touch Pad to Camera, close phone in car + spam Touch Pad | Rebind View Button to Camera, close phone in car + spam View Button | Yes, on foot, driving, and combat | Reload a save or quit the game |

## How to Exit First-Person Mode Without Losing Progress

Because this is an unintended glitch, cycling your camera button normally or even dying will not switch you back to third-person. You do not need to revert to an old save, though: simply create a manual save while in first-person and immediately reload that exact save. The game will keep your progress and restore the standard third-person camera.

## Pro Tips & What to Watch Out For

- **Make a backup save first:** Always save on a separate slot before experimenting with camera glitches in case an animation or mission transition behaves strangely.
- **Expect a few tries:** The timing window is tight, so if the camera snaps back to third-person, pull your phone out again and repeat the Circle/B plus camera-spam sequence.
- **Use iron sights on foot:** Gunplay works surprisingly well in this glitched mode, and clicking the right thumbstick lets you zoom and aim down the sights of your weapons.
- **Expect visual jank:** This view is unfinished—your character's shoulders and arms can clip into the camera with long guns, and animations like breaking into vehicles look rough.
- **Rockstar could patch it:** Since this is a glitch rather than an official feature, a future patch could remove it. Rockstar has not confirmed whether an official first-person mode is planned.
