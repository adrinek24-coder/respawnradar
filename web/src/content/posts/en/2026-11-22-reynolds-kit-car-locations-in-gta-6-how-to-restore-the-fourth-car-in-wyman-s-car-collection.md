To restore the Reynolds Kit Car in GTA 6, you need to track down five wrecked shells across Leonida, scan each one on foot with the WAiNK app, and pay Wyman $1,500 once he texts you. This is the fourth vehicle in Wyman's Classic Car Collection—keep in mind the entire questline is exclusive to the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition, and one wreck is story-locked behind the Cut-Off Tool from Chapter 3.

## Requirements Before You Start Hunting

- **Ultimate Edition only:** Wyman's project car questline (Motel Scrapper) does not appear in the Standard Edition.
- **Unlock the questline:** Meet Wyman in Chapter 2 and finish the Thrillbillies main mission. He texts you a waypoint to the Paradise RV Park garage to pick up the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy; shortly after, he starts messaging you about classic wrecks to scan.
- **Scanning controls:** On foot, hold D-Pad Up to open the WAiNK app, then hit R1 (PlayStation) or RB (Xbox) to scan the wreck. You pocket $50 via BuckMe per scan.
- **Chapter 3 tool gate:** Grab the Cut-Off Tool (angle grinder) during Chapter 3 before heading to Wild Hog Point, or you won't be able to pop the shipping container lock.

## All 5 Reynolds Kit Car Wreck Locations

You can scan these five wrecks in any order you like:

- **1. Wild Hog Point (Beach Shipping Container):** Head west of Port Gellhorn to Wild Hog Point, drive to the end of the airstrip, and turn right onto the sand road heading toward the beach (east of the Wild Hog Point marker near the shallowest point of the lake). Look for the standalone yellow-orange Scoop shipping container near the water, cut it open with your Cut-Off Tool, and scan the wreck inside.
- **2. Capri (Northwest Mansion Garage):** Drive to the northwest residential finger of Capri on the far western edge of the map. Right as the road bends into an L-shape near a construction site, look across the street for a white marble mansion with wood trim (and two knocked-over traffic cones plus two bins out front). Head up the driveway and break into the locked garage via the garage door or side door to scan the rusty Reynolds.
- **3. Watson Bay (Behind Donnie's Auto Repair):** Go south of Grassrivers to Watson Bay in Mariana County and cruise along Seabell Avenue near the southern Pay 'n' Spray and Ammu-Nation. Across from the big motel jutting into the water sits a closed shop called Donnie's Auto Repair—follow the wide gravel path to its left and wrap around the back to find the wreck sitting out in the open.
- **4. Casanova Island, Rialto Islands (Mansion Driveway):** Head to Casanova Island, one of the three adjoining Rialto Islands linking Vice City and Vice Beach. Follow the northern/middle strip of road until you hit a vacant lot, walk to the mansion immediately to its left, and simply push open the brown driveway gate—no lockpicking needed, the wreck is sitting right there.
- **5. Salton / East of VCIA (Willie's Service Center):** Head to southern Salton in Vice City (Vice-Dale County, east of VCIA airport), north of the 7th Avenue Garage and just north of the small street between a pawn shop and Ammu-Nation. West of the tall green parking garage sits an orange/brown-and-blue repair shop called Willie's Service Center. The bay doors are shut, so smash open the side door to get inside. Heads up: breaking in trips a security alarm, so scan fast and bail out.

## How to Claim the Restored Reynolds Kit Car

- Once all five scans are sent over, Wyman confirms he can rebuild the car.
- Go knock out a side activity or free-roam for a bit until Wyman texts asking for $1,500 to cover parts and labor.
- Send him the $1,500 directly from the message.
- Wait for the pickup notification, then drive over to Wyman's scrapyard (the W icon on your map) to collect your restored Reynolds Kit Car.

## Pro Tips and Missable Warnings

- **Stage your getaway at Willie's Service Center:** Before smashing that side door in Salton, park your ride facing the highway. The security alarm goes off the second you break in, so hit the WAiNK scan and bolt before heat shows up.
- **Track missing scans easily:** Lost count of which wrecks you grabbed? Open the Progress menu, go to Exploration, and check the Collections tab to verify your exact progress.
- **Don't panic if you wreck or abandon it:** Even if you don't register or garage the Reynolds Kit Car right away, you can swing back to Wyman's scrapyard anytime to pick up any of his restored rides again.
- **Unlocking the fifth and final car:** Finishing the Reynolds Kit Car (along with the '67 Transgressor, '70 Riata, and '64 Mamba GT) triggers Wyman's call for the fifth bonus car in the collection, the '72 Deviant (Born Free Deviant).
