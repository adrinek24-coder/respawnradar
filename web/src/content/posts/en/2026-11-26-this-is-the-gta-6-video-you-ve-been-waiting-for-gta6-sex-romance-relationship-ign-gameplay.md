To unlock every intimate cutscene and max out Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos's romance in GTA 6, you need to push their hidden Relationship Level to **Crazy in Love (3/4)** or **Hot Together (4/4)** through daily two-way interactions—main story missions alone won't get you there. You can check their current standing anytime by holding **Right on the D-Pad** (watch the heart icon in the bottom right) or opening the **Jason & Lucia** menu in the GTA 6 Index.

## How to Reach Hot Together Fast (Exact Controls)

Whenever you score points with your partner, a small **pink +Heart icon** flashes on screen. There's no single infinite grind, and you have to **switch between Jason and Lucia regularly**—one-sided effort stalls your progress. Mix these into your normal free-roam routine:

- **Hold hands while walking:** Stay close to your partner and hold **R2 (PS5) / RT (Xbox)**.
- **Hug, kiss & check in:** Focus on your partner with **L2 / LT**, then hit **Square / X** (or the on-screen heart prompt).
- **Share food and drinks:** While holding food or a drink, hold **L2 / LT** to hand some over.
- **Open the car door:** Hold **Triangle / Y** next to your personal vehicle to open the door for them.
- **Bring them on jobs & hangouts:** Hold **Left on the D-Pad** and press **Triangle / Y** to invite your partner to robberies or dates. Hitting the gym together, dancing at clubs (hold L2/LT close together), and walking the beach all stack easy hearts.
- **Don't ghost on WhatUp:** Answer texts promptly, send 'Check In' messages, and actually come home to sleep together after long outings.

## How to Trigger Intimate Scenes (Outside & Inside)

Once you're at **Crazy in Love** or **Hot Together**, you can trigger scenes in three ways. Expect topless moments and skin rather than full-frontal nudity—even at **Hot Together**, Rockstar keeps things firmly in M-rated territory:

- **Outside the safehouse:** After hanging out together for a while, one of them may suggest heading home. Focus with **L2 / LT** and pick the **house + heart** prompt. That auto-sets a waypoint to your safehouse, and walking through the front door triggers the cutscene.
- **Inside the safehouse:** Depending on what your partner is doing (bed, couch, or shower), focus with **L2 / LT** and watch for the **two hearts** interact prompt. Choosing to sleep next to each other can also kick off a scene.
- **Story triggers:** Several story missions naturally end with Jason and Lucia getting intimate if your bond is high.
- **Respect the cooldown:** After a scene plays, a cooldown of at least a couple of real-life hours kicks in before you can trigger another one, and your partner can occasionally turn you down.

## Pro Tips & Missable Warnings

- **Don't skip Chapter 1 prep tasks:** Before picking Lucia up in the Chapter 1 mission 'Coming Home', knock out Jason's optional chores—fix up the safehouse, grab groceries, buy flowers, pick up a necklace at *The Siren's Chest*, get a haircut (*Get The Chop*), collect her clothes, and show up on time. Doing these starts you at **Crazy in Love (3/4)** instead of **Partners in Crime (2/4)**. They vanish once Lucia gets home, so drop a manual save first.
- **Split your playtime evenly:** Players who stay glued to Jason all campaign struggle to hit **Hot Together**. Swap to Lucia regularly and trigger affection prompts from her side too.
- **Watch your Criminal Profile:** If either protagonist's Criminal Profile sinks to **Irredeemable**, your relationship can take a hit that's brutal or impossible to recover from.
- **Best Ending checklist:** Locking in GTA 6's best ending requires **Hot Together (4/4)**, **Professional** Criminal Profiles on both leads, and **over $2 million** in savings before heading into the finale.
