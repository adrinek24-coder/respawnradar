To get a **Poop Bag** and throw dog poop at NPCs in GTA 6, head to **Vice Beach**, wait for a pedestrian's dog to do its business and get bagged by the owner, then punch or hit the owner with another object so they drop the bag. Walk over the dropped bag, press **Triangle (PS5) / Y (Xbox)** to grab it, and either aim and hurl it or smack an NPC in the face up close.

## Step-by-Step: How to Grab and Use the Poop Bag

- **Head to Vice Beach:** Any dog walker in Leonida works, but crowded **Vice Beach** in Vice City spawns plenty of pedestrians walking dogs right near each other, saving you a ton of wandering.
- **Don't crowd the owner too early:** Trail the dog walker at a normal distance. GTA 6 NPCs clock you if you stalk or stare at them for too long, and some owners will pick a fight or set their dog on you before the dog even squats.
- **Wait for the scoop:** You cannot scoop dog poop off the sand yourself. Let the dog finish and wait until the owner bends down and picks it up into a **Poop Bag**.
- **Force the drop:** Once the bag is in the NPC's hand, shove or punch them (**Square on PS5 / X on Xbox** on Default controls) or toss a snack or small world prop at them to knock the bag loose.
- **Pick up the Poop Bag:** Step up to the dropped bag until the **Poop Bag** prompt pops up, then press **Triangle (PS5) / Y (Xbox)** to grab it.
- **Throw or face-smash:** Hold **L2 / LT** or the throw trigger (**R2 / RT**) to aim—click **R3 / RS** while aiming if you want a visible throwing arc—and let it fly. If you want the grossest reaction, walk right up and press melee (**Square / X**) to smash the bag directly into someone's face, bursting it on impact and making them recoil.

## Pro Tips & Warnings Before Starting a Beach Feud

- **Civilians pack heat in Leonida:** The dog walker knows it was you. Depending on their temperament, they may square up, try to disarm you, call the cops, or pull a concealed firearm. Do not assume a dog-walking tourist is harmless.
- **Watch your Criminal Profile:** Jason and Lucia have separate Criminal Profiles (*Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho, Irredeemable*). Throwing a bag and running off is harmless, but turning the brawl into a civilian massacre or killing the dog tanks your rating. Once a character hits **Irredeemable**, it is permanent—locking you out of Focus bonuses and police surrenders. Hold **D-Pad Right** for a quick status icon, or open the map and press **L1 / LB** to inspect the *Jason & Lucia* tab.
- **Smell reactions & calming dogs:** Nearby beachgoers react to the foul smell thanks to GTA 6's smell system. If the dog gets aggressive during the scuffle and you don't want a Criminal Profile hit for hurting an animal, buy a **10-pack of pet treats** from a convenience store cashier, lock onto the dog with **L2 / LT**, and toss a treat to calm it down.
- **Using Classic controls?** If you switched from *Default* to *Classic* controls, melee moves to **Circle / B** while interact stays on **Triangle / Y**, so follow the on-screen button prompt when grabbing the bag.
