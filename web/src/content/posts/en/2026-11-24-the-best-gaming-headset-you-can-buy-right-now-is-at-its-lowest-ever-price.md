The SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro Omni just hit its lowest-ever price for Black Friday 2026: $344.99 on Amazon for the black colorway (down from $399.99 MSRP, beating its old record low by $15). If you bounce between PC, PS5, and Xbox and want one wireless headset that never dies mid-match, this is the deal to grab.

## Buying Checklist & Day-1 Setup Steps

Don't let messy store listings or default EQ settings ruin a $345 purchase—run through these steps first:

- **Pick the black colorway for the lowest price:** Amazon's $344.99 record low applies only to the black model; the white and blue finishes cost $5 more.
- **Double-check for Omni in the title:** Retailers are also discounting the older Arctis Nova Pro Wireless, which only has two USB ports, splits Xbox and PlayStation SKUs, and lacks wireless Hi-Res audio.
- **Hook up all three systems at once:** Plug your PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S into the GameHub's three USB-C ports. OmniPlay lets you blend up to four audio sources simultaneously, including Bluetooth from your phone.
- **Charge the spare battery right away:** Pop the magnetic plate off the right earcup to slot in battery #1 and slide battery #2 into the GameHub charger. That gives you 50+ combined hours with ANC on and zero cable downtime.
- **Skip the aggressive game presets:** Use SteelSeries Sonar on PC or the Arctis Mobile App on consoles, start from the **Flat** profile, and tweak from there.

## Honest Pro Tips & Easy-to-Miss Catches

- **No 3.5mm cable in the box:** Even though the left earcup includes a 3.5mm jack, you have to bring your own analog cable if you want zero-latency wired play.
- **Watch the treble on Sonar presets:** SteelSeries' neodymium drivers already lean a bit sharp up top, and several game-specific presets exaggerate that scratchiness. Dialing down upper treble slightly from Flat sounds cleaner in both shooters and music.
- **Bluetooth codec limitation:** You get SBC, LC3, and LC3+ over Bluetooth 5.3, but zero AAC or aptX support. Stick to the 2.4GHz GameHub for gaming and keep Bluetooth for Discord or podcasts.
- **ANC is built for rooms, not windy walks:** Hybrid ANC does a solid job muting PC fans and street rumble, though strong wind outdoors causes audible crackling.
- **Warm earpads & fit check:** At 339g the Omni feels super plush, but the faux-leather cushions trap heat in warm rooms and can feel snug if you have larger ears. And if you only game on one platform and don't need a desktop hub, $345 is overkill—mid-range Black Friday picks like the HyperX Cloud III S Wireless ($99.69) or Arctis Nova 3 Wireless save you serious cash.
