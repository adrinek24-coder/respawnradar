## How to Redeem Slap Volleyball Codes

There is no level gate, group requirement, or gamepass needed to claim codes. Redeem them straight from the lobby:

- Launch **Slap Volleyball** (by Chamagic) on Roblox and wait until your character spawns in.
- Click the **Gear icon** in the top-right corner of the screen to open Settings.
- Select the **ENTER A CODE** button at the bottom of the Settings menu.
- Type or paste a working code into the **TYPE IT HERE** box.
- Hit **REDEEM** to add the Coins and Slap Crate directly to your inventory.

## Pro Tips & Missable Warnings

- **Don't mix up games:** Slap Volleyball is separate from *Slap* and *Slap Battles*. Codes from *Slap* like `isthatall` or `ultimatedelay` will always fail here.
- **Cosmetics only:** Coins and Slap Crates unlock explosions, ball trails, gloves, jerseys, accessories, and titles—they never boost your slap power or movement speed.
- **Check error messages before giving up:** Seeing *"That code doesn't exist. Check the spelling!"* usually means a typo or a stray space after pasting, whereas *"That code has expired"* confirms the code is dead.
- **Claim fast:** Codes rotate out quickly, just like `LAUNCH` did, so grab `RANKED` and `SLAP` before the next patch drops.
- **Ranked queue unlock tip:** The `RANKED` code celebrates the 1v1 and 2v2 Ranked queue (real players only, no bots), which unlocks after 10 finished matches and includes 3 placement matches where you cannot lose rank.
