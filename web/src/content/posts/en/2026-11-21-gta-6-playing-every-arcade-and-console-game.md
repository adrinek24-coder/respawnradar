## Quick Answer (TL;DR)

Grand Theft Auto VI features **4 fully playable video games**: **Tenebrae Lux**, **Street Trash III: Dezombifier**, and **Time Jackers: Rewind** are played on the **JoySystem** home console after unlocking it during the Chapter 1 story mission *Feng Shui*. The fourth game, **Bang For Your Buck**, is exclusive to **arcade cabinets** around Leonida—your easiest spots are outside **Bargain World** or inside **Game Horde** in Port Gellhorn. For **100% completion**, simply start each of the 4 titles once and quit. To earn the **Stick Fiddler** bronze trophy/achievement fast, beat all 3 levels of *Bang For Your Buck* in about 10–15 minutes.

## Step-by-Step: How to Unlock & Play All 4 Games

- **Unlock the JoySystem in Chapter 1:** Progress through the story until the mission **Feng Shui**, where Jason and Cal arrange the living room and sit down to play. You automatically sample *Tenebrae Lux* and a second console game during this sequence.
- **Launch the remaining console game:** Walk up to the controller near the TV in Jason's house and boot up any JoySystem title you haven't played yet.
- **Find a Bang For Your Buck arcade cabinet:** Head to Port Gellhorn and check the cabinet outside the **Bargain World** clothing store or walk inside **Game Horde** (located immediately on your right). Other confirmed locations include **Rusty Anchor**, **Xero Food Mart** in southern Kelly County, **Richardson Jewelry & Diamonds** in Downtown Vice City, and **Electric Boulevard** at Stockyard in Vice City.
- **Verify your 100% checklist:** Open the pause menu and check **Progress** to confirm all four games are logged. Loading into a game and exiting right away counts.
- **Clear one game for Stick Fiddler:** Finish every stage of any single game—*Bang For Your Buck* is the shortest and most forgiving pick.

## Gameplay Breakdown of Every Mini-Game

### Bang For Your Buck (Best for Stick Fiddler)
An energetic lightgun-style arcade shooter spanning 3 levels where you hunt bucks, critters, and bonus crates while avoiding female deer (does), which penalize your score. Your cash counter ticks down continuously when idle, so stay aggressive. Two helpful tricks make *Stick Fiddler* effortless: replaying earlier stages carries your accumulated funds forward, and Stage 3 has no fixed score threshold—just prevent your counter from hitting zero before **Stage Complete** appears. Perfectionists can also chase 5 built-in challenges like A++ Grades in every stage or rifle-only B-grade clears.

### Street Trash III: Dezombifier
Set during a zombie outbreak in Freedom City, this isometric extraction shooter plays like a compact roguelike. Manage health and stamina as you kite undead mobs, earn XP to choose 1 of 3 Perk Cards per level-up (offering new weapons, fire-rate boosts, health regen, or extra XP, with up to 3 re-rolls), and gather **6 randomly spawned gears** to power the extraction lift. Aside from your safehouse console, it also appears on select open-world cabinets in areas like Watson Bay, Hamlet, and Vice City.

### Time Jackers: Rewind
Step into the boots of Captain Jack Hoffman in a futuristic cyberpunk city armed with a laser rifle. Scavenge ammo drops, tap **L1** to cycle weapons once you hold more than one, and loot chrono-power charges from downed foes to slow time. Balance survival with speed to reach the exit before the top-left timer bar expires.

### Tenebrae Lux
Rockstar's sharp satire of moody indie walking simulators drops you on a foggy shoreline beneath a lighthouse. Head into the lit shack on the left to pick up a board game and an 11-page manual whose pages unlock gradually. Shift pieces and ram pyramid tokens into immovable square blocks without breaking your pyramids, leading up to a final bunker puzzle with 6 randomized steps.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Don't panic about the Chapter 2 lockout:** If you forgot to launch the third JoySystem game in Chapter 1, it is **not missable**. Console access disappears during Chapter 2 (while at the Starlet Motel), then returns permanently in Chapter 3 and throughout the post-game.
- **Check the cabinet marquee first:** Not all 31 arcade machines across Leonida run *Bang For Your Buck*—many run *Street Trash III: Dezombifier* instead. Go straight to **Game Horde** or **Bargain World** in Port Gellhorn to save travel time.
- **Don't overcomplicate 100% completion:** You do not need to solve *Tenebrae Lux*'s randomized bunker puzzle or grind high scores unless you want to. Boot all 4 games once for the checklist, then wrap up *Stick Fiddler* on *Bang For Your Buck*.
