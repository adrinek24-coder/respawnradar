## Quick Answer / TL;DR

**Bodycam**'s ultra-realistic Zombies mode returned on November 13, 2026 with free update **v0.9 The Dead & Damned**—and following huge player demand, developer Reissad Studio confirmed the mode is now **permanent**. Up to four players fight through pitch-black horror with zero HUD across three maps (**Zombie Trenches**, **Village**, and **Asylum**), earning **Reissad Points** while looting randomized attachment boxes and new **Loadout Boxes** featuring the brand-new **TK-9** machine pistol and **Crossbow**.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Surviving Bodycam Zombies

With realistic body-camera sway, scarce ammo, and no HUD safety net, treating Bodycam Zombies like an arcade shooter will get your squad wiped fast:

- **Prioritize Weapon & Loadout Boxes early:** Mystery boxes randomize their locations every match. Standard weapon crates roll guns with random attachments (usually including a flashlight), while the special red **Loadout Box** drops your exact equipped primary and secondary weapons.
- **Bring the new v0.9 loadout gear:** Equip the new full-auto **TK-9** secondary for panic close-quarters defense and test the reworked **Crossbow**—previously locked behind the Village secret, it now supports attachments and has reduced arrow drop.
- **Play around squad respawn rules:** In co-op, downed teammates automatically respawn at the start of the next completed wave as long as one player survives; in solo mode, dying ends your run immediately.
- **How to beat Zombie Trenches (10-Minute Holdout):** Jump and vault are enabled here, but avoid getting cornered inside narrow trench mud. Grab a 30-round rifle and hold elevated choke points like the Tank hardpoint or the fallen tree near the stairs, where zombies funnel slowly for easy headshots.
- **How to beat Village (Easter Egg Escape):** Keep early wave counts low while powering the generators. Defeat the cornfield and cemetery bosses to grab the hidden key spawning from a grave, enter the church underground via the pressure plate, and **crouch inside the mine cart** so the low cave ceiling does not insta-kill you. Use the Crossbow to blow open the jailhouse safe, collect both gears, unlock the barn basement near the windmill to read the directional code (East/West), and input that sequence in the church.
- **How to survive Asylum:** Activate generators floor by floor to unlock sealed sections and take the elevator down to the basement security booth. One player operates the security cameras and door controls while teammates protect the booth and activate cameras across the building (note: hotfix **v0.9.1** on November 16, 2026 fixed the Asylum elevator softlock).

## Pro Tips & What to Watch Out For

- **Drop screaming sprinters first:** Every fifth wave—plus special map events—spawns screeching sprinters that lunge from surprising distances and shred squads in seconds. Crank up a solid stereo or surround headset because directional audio is your primary early-warning system.
- **Stagger your reloads:** Never reload at the same time as your lane partner. Call out reloads over voice chat, conserve ammo with deliberate headshots, and remember you still carry a melee knife for desperate empty-mag moments.
- **Watch for flashlight-less box rolls:** Most box weapons include tactical lights, but occasionally a gun drops without one—avoid swapping into a dark weapon inside Asylum or the Village tunnels.
- **Optimize your PC settings:** Minimum specs require Windows 10/11 64-bit, Ryzen 7 3700X / i7-9700K, 8 GB RAM, RX 5700 / RTX 2070 (8 GB), and 50 GB storage on an SSD. If Zombies drops to 40–50 FPS, turn on **NVIDIA DLSS** or **AMD FSR** upscaling in the video settings.
- **Party system still missing in v0.9:** While v0.9 launched **Easy Anti-Cheat**, a reworked **Public Pool** PvP map, and a lobby **Drone & RC Car** room, the full party system was delayed again and remains a top roadmap priority heading toward 2027.
