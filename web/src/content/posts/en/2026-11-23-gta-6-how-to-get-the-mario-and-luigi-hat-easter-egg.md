Head straight to the crumbling brick ruins of the **Lawton Co. Paper Mill** in southwestern **Moreau, Lummox County**, hit the bong on the purple tarp, climb to the top planks by the waterfall, and smash (or shoot) the floating transparent cube to unlock the Mario- and Luigi-themed **"J" and "L" Baseball Caps** for Jason and Lucia.

## Step-by-Step: How to Get Both Hats

- **Drive to the ruins:** Pull up your map and go north of **Mount Kalaga** into **Lummox County**. Look southwest of **Moreau** right next to **Adler Creek**, just north of the West Kalaga 24/7 gas station, for the **Lawton Co. Paper Mill** ruins. It's also marked as a **WAiNK landmark**, and a meditation spot pops up when you get close.
- **Hit the bong:** Inside the riverside ruins near the small waterfall and graffiti wall, look for a **purple tarp** (near a table) with a bong sitting on it. Walk up and hit the **Smoke** prompt.
- **Spot the invisible block:** Once the trip kicks in, your visuals warp and your Focus overpowers. Look up toward the plume of smoke rising above the tallest ruin column near the waterfall—a **semi-transparent floating cube** fades into the air.
- **Climb the ruins:** Parkour up the broken brick wall onto the wooden planks and keep climbing until you're standing right underneath the cube.
- **Break the cube:** Jump straight up (or jump-punch) into the bottom of the block like Mario. If the jump refuses to connect after a couple of tries, **just pull out a gun and shoot the cube** instead.
- **Grab the caps:** A short nostalgic tune plays and the cube bursts, dropping the **"J" Baseball Cap** (Jason) and **"L" Baseball Cap** (Lucia). Pick them up, then swap into them at a clothing store or wardrobe.

## Pro Tips & What's Easy to Mess Up

- **Scout the climb while sober:** The bong trip is timed. Do a quick dry run up the bricks and wooden planks before smoking so you aren't fumbling jumps while the screen is warping.
- **Skip the janky jump hitbox:** Lining up the jump under the cube is notoriously finicky. If two jumps whiff, whip out a pistol or SMG and shoot the block—it triggers the exact same burst and reward drop.
- **Check the ground below the planks:** Most players get both caps dropping together, though at least one solo PS5 report only saw Jason's "J" cap land on the planks. Drop down and scan around the base near the waterfall in case a cap tumbled off the narrow ledge.
- **Loot the area while you're there:** Before leaving the paper mill ruins, sweep the riverbank for extra hidden cash and grab the WAiNK landmark check-in.
