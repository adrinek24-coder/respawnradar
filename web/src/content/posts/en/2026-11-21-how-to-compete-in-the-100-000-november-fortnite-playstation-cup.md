To enter the $100,000 November 2026 Fortnite PlayStation Cup, boot up Fortnite on your PS4 or PS5, check your regional start window in the Compete tab, and queue directly into the PlayStation Cup Solo playlist when Round 1 opens on Monday, November 23. Epic is running two separate Solo competitions—Battle Royale and Zero Build—splitting $50,000 per mode across seven regions, with the Top 80 players from each Qualifier advancing to the Finals.

## How to Enter & Eligibility Checklist

Sort out your account setup before Monday so you aren't locked out of the playlist at kickoff:

- **Console Lock:** PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5 only.
- **Linked Account & 2FA:** Your PSN account must be linked to your Epic Games account, with Two-Factor Authentication (2FA/MFA) enabled.
- **Account Level 50+:** Your Epic account must be in good standing and at least Level 50 (verify this inside the Career tab).
- **Age & Rules:** You must be at least 13 years old (or higher if required by your country); players under 18 need a parent or legal guardian to accept the Official Rules.
- **Queueing Up:** Open Fortnite on PlayStation, visit the **Compete** tab to confirm exact session times for your region, then switch to the **Play** tab, open Mode Selection, and select the **PlayStation Cup** playlist once live.

## Schedule, Format & Free Spray Reward

Both Qualifiers happen on the same day, followed by back-to-back Finals days:

- **Monday, November 23 (Round 1 – Battle Royale & Zero Build):** Roughly a 3-hour window with a cap of **11 matches**. Matches only count if they start before the window closes, and the **Top 80** players per region qualify for Finals.
- **8-Point Participation Reward:** Earning at least **8 points** in Round 1 of either mode unlocks the **Froggory Stance** Spray.
- **Tuesday, November 24 (Zero Build Finals):** Roughly 2 hours and 40 minutes long, capped at **6 scheduled matches**.
- **Wednesday, November 25 (Battle Royale Finals):** Same 6-match scheduled format as Zero Build Finals.
- **Tiebreakers:** Ties break in strict order by total points, total Victory Royales, average eliminations, average placement, and total seconds survived.

## Pro Tips & Missable Warnings

- **Commit to one mode on Nov 23:** Battle Royale and Zero Build Qualifiers run simultaneously in the same 3-hour block for your region. Splitting games between both usually kills your Top 80 pace—pick your stronger mode and burn all 11 games smartly. (If you only care about the Froggory Stance spray, grab 8 quick points in either playlist and you're done.)
- **Watch the Finals clock like a hawk:** Finals matches don't let you queue whenever you want. Game 1 launches **10 minutes** after the session opens, followed by a new lobby roughly every **30 minutes**. Miss a launch window and you get slapped with last place and 0 elims.
- **Lock in 120 FPS & Ethernet on PS5:** You're strictly matched against PS4 and PS5 players, so turning on 120 FPS Mode (if your display supports 120Hz) and running a wired connection gives you a legit edge in laggy moving zones.
- **Don't touch your Epic account after placing:** If you finish in the money, Epic requires eligibility verification plus tax and payment forms before payout—keep your Epic account active and watch your email so you don't forfeit earnings.
