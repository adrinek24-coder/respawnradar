Skip aimless window-shopping across Leonida's 67 clothing stores and head straight to the spots that actually carry standout gear: **Ferruccio Tucci** for luxury suits and bodysuits, **Aline's Thrift Store** and **Dr. Froogal** for rocker and leather jackets, **Hamlet Thrift** for Lucia's cowboy boots, and specific salons like **Clip & Fade**, **Pearl Hair Salon**, and **Salon Belle** for signature hairstyles, nails, and makeup.

## How to Build the Best Looks Fast

- Open your map, go to **Map > Style**, and filter by Clothing, Hair & Beauty, or Tattoos—every style shop in Leonida is open 24/7 once you clear the map fog in that district.
- Hit only one branch of any big chain (Ferruccio Tucci, Slinger, Mid, This / That, Nola, Alpha Sports), since every store under the same brand sells identical inventory.
- Prioritize the 5 standalone thrift stores—especially **Aline's Thrift Store**, **Dr. Froogal**, **Hamlet Thrift**, and **Bargain World**—because they hold unique jackets, boots, and vintage pieces you cannot buy at chains.
- Always check the **Signature** tab at salons instead of sticking to default cuts; viral Lucia looks like Spiky Twin Buns and Goddess Braids are locked to specific stylists.
- Save up to 15 complete looks in your safehouse wardrobe and register 5 of them to your vehicle trunk so both protagonists can change clothes on the fly after jobs.

## Pro Tips & Missable Details

- Touch up Lucia's makeup before launching major story missions—if she stays awake too long without refreshing it, her makeup noticeably smudges during cutscenes.
- **Salon Belle** in Rockridge is tucked inside a residential block rather than facing a main road, so walk inside the building complex when the map pin looks buried.
- Don't waste time looking for socks or motorcycle helmets in stores: socks cannot be purchased anywhere, and motorcycle helmets require toggling **Auto Equip Helmet** on in Settings.
- Check **Ammu-Nation** gun stores and **Mod Shops** that display a T-shirt icon under Features on the map—they stock tactical pieces and extra gear missing from regular fashion shops.
- Use outfits tactically after robberies: wear a hat, glasses, and mask going in, strip all three off once you break line of sight (your partner mirrors you automatically), then swap outfits at the trunk of any registered car to shake police heat.
- Boost Jason and Lucia's relationship status by getting matching couples tattoos like the **'In this Life...' / '...and the next'** set—just keep in mind one matching design is locked behind a side quest.
