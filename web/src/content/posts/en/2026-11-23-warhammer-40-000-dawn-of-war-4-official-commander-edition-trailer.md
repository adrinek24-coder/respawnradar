Buy the Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV Commander Edition ($80.99 with the 10% Steam pre-order discount, $89.99 regular) only if you care about jumping in 3 days early on November 30 and already plan to grab both paid Year 1 story expansions. If you just want classic base-building RTS action, four launch campaigns, and the returning Crusade Mode, stick to the Standard Edition ($53.99 pre-order, $59.99 regular) — Crusade Mode and the Mission Editor are free for everyone.

## What You Actually Get in the Commander Edition

The official Commander Edition trailer packs a lot of hype into a couple minutes, so here is the exact split of what your extra $27 (at pre-order pricing) actually pays for:

- **Base Game at Launch (4 Factions):** Command Space Marines (Blood Ravens and Dark Angels, featuring playable Primarch Lion El'Jonson), Orks, Necrons, and series newcomers Adeptus Mechanicus. Launch includes 70+ branching campaign missions (solo or co-op), Skirmish, Last Stand, 1v1/2v2/3v3 PvP, and the Army Painter.
- **3-Day Advance Access:** Unlock the game on Monday, November 30, 2026, instead of Thursday, December 3.
- **Blood Ravens Story Prologue DLC (Spring 2027):** Paid story expansion covering events leading into the main Kronus campaign.
- **Aftermath Campaign Expansion (Summer 2027):** Paid post-campaign expansion introducing an unannounced fifth playable faction.
- **Digital Soundtrack (OST):** Official downloadable game soundtrack.

## Pro Tips & Missable Buying Warnings

- **Don't buy Commander Edition just for Crusade Mode:** A lot of players mix up the free roadmap with the Year 1 paid bundle. Crusade Mode, Map Packs 1–4, the Mission Editor, and free Last Stand updates drop for Standard Edition owners too.
- **You're buying the 5th faction blind:** Deep Silver hasn't revealed which army arrives in Summer 2027's Aftermath expansion, and exact DLC release dates aren't locked in yet.
- **Use early access as a free review window:** The 10% pre-purchase discount runs right up until December 3. If you're unsure about performance after the delay from September, watch Commander Edition impressions from November 30 to December 2, then grab the Standard Edition at 10% off before launch day ends.
- **Clear SSD space ahead of time:** Initial install sits around 100 GB (roughly 25 GB is just high-res cutscenes), with 125 GB listed to leave breathing room for future patches and DLC.
- **Aging rig? GeForce NOW is confirmed:** If your GPU is below a GTX 1660, you can stream your Steam copy through NVIDIA GeForce NOW instead of panic-upgrading.
