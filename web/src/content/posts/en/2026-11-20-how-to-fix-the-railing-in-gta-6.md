## Quick Answer: Where to Find the Broken Railing

To fix the railing at Jason's Key Lento safehouse in GTA 6, exit the living room through the back sliding door, walk down the stairs on the left into the outdoor gym and BBQ area, and face the house next to the silver ladder and weights. Look for the paintbrush interact icon on the missing gap in the railing, hold **Triangle (PS5)** or **Y (Xbox Series X|S)**, and press **R2 / RT** to nail the new boards into place.

## Step-by-Step: How to Fix the Railing in GTA 6

The broken railing is easily the trickiest of Jason's Chapter 1 house projects because the game only lets you trigger it from one side. Follow these exact steps:

- Walk through Jason's house and open the sliding glass door to step out onto the back deck/veranda.
- Do not try to interact with the railing from the upper deck closest to the back door—the prompt will not appear from that side.
- Turn left and head down the stairs to the lower outdoor gym area with the BBQ, kettlebells, squat rack, and bench press.
- Turn back to face the house and walk up to the gap in the wooden railing right between the metal ladder and the workout equipment.
- Approach the gap from the gym side until the paintbrush icon pops up, then hold **Triangle (PS5)** or **Y (Xbox Series X|S)** to start the job.
- Tap **R2 (PS5)** or **RT (Xbox Series X|S)** as prompted to use the nail gun and secure the new wooden rails.

## All Jason's House Repairs in Key Lento (Checklist & Controls)

You can track your progress anytime by opening the map and selecting Jason's House under the Optional list. All five checklist tasks take place across four spots around Brian's rent-free waterfront property:

| Repair Task | Exact Location | Interact Object | Controls (PS5 / Xbox) | Cost & Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fix Steps | Front exterior stairs near driveway, mailbox & basketball hoop | Stack of wooden 2x4 planks beside the stairs | Remove: L2+R2 then tap X / LT+RT then tap A; Nail: R2 / RT | Free; Lucia relationship boost |
| Fix Leaky Roof | Roof above the front porch | Climb yellow ladder outside front door to blue tarp/shingles | Nail gun: R2 / RT | Free; Lucia relationship boost |
| Fix Railing | Back patio outdoor gym (lower level) | Missing railing gap next to silver ladder & weights | Start: Hold Triangle / Y; Nail gun: R2 / RT | Free; Lucia relationship boost |
| Fix Wall & Paint Wall | Jason's bedroom (opposite the bed) | Stacked drywall panels next to the pedestal fan | Drill: Hold R2 / RT; Paint: Left Stick | Free; Lucia relationship boost |

## Pro Tips & What to Watch Out For

- **Approach strictly from the gym side:** If you stand on the top veranda trying to line up the prompt, nothing will happen. Always walk down the back stairs to the weights first.
- **Finish everything before picking up Lucia:** While some walkthroughs note you can still repair the house after Lucia returns, other guides warn that the house projects become permanently missable once you go pick her up from prison. Finish all four areas early in Chapter 1 to guarantee the early welcome bonus.
- **Pick your bedroom paint color:** Right after screwing in the bedroom drywall, Jason lets you roll on a fresh coat of paint in your choice of Tropical Green, Stormy Blue, or Pastel Peach—and there is no waiting for it to dry.
- **Zero cost, fast relationship boost:** You do not need to buy materials or visit a hardware store. Each completed job pops a Heart icon on screen, strengthening Jason and Lucia's relationship early on.
