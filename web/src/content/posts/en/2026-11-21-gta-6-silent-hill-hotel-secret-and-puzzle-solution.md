**Quick Answer / TL;DR:** GTA 6's Silent Hill-inspired secret takes place inside the abandoned **Marbelle Hotel** in southern Crosstown, northern Vice City. Slip in through the rear entrance, grab the **Purple Ratface Comic Collectible Card** on the lower floor, trigger the paranormal sequence upstairs, and collect the **Old Note** on the foggy rooftop. Enter the six-digit code **955214** into the downstairs safe to score several thousand dollars, the **Crowex Ambassador 'Breakfast' Collectible Watch**, and **The Truth Is Out There** trophy.

## Where to Find the Haunted Marbelle Hotel

Head to **southern Crosstown**, a neighborhood in northern Vice City within Vice-Dale County. Look along **NW 8th Street** near the interstate, close to the **Nine1Nine** nightclub and west of **Panos Plaza**, for an abandoned hotel with green awnings and a For Sale sign. Because the main front door is boarded up, circle around the exterior to the back of the building and step inside through the unlocked rear door.

## Step-by-Step Silent Hill Puzzle Solution

- **Loot the lower floor first:** Inside on the ground floor, note the locked safe tucked near the stairs—you will come back to it shortly. Before heading up, inspect the cabinet opposite the staircase to pick up the **Death of Swallow — Purple Ratface Comic Collectible Card**.
- **Explore upstairs:** Walk up the stairs and check out the unsettling Silent Hill references, including a mannequin facing a corner with a lead pipe leaning nearby and another mannequin on a bed next to ominous warnings.
- **Trigger the haunting:** When a door at the top of the stairs swings open by itself, walk through it. The door slams shut behind you, the lights cut out, eerie whispers fill the stairwell, and glowing red symbols and graffiti—including GET OUT! and the safe digits—cover the walls and ceiling.
- **Step out onto the rooftop:** Wait out the sequence until the lights return and the rooftop door opens. Walking outside transforms the Vice City skyline into thick, Silent Hill-style fog.
- **Find the Old Note:** Search the rooftop ground for a collectible document called the **Old Note**. This rushed realtor listing explains the building's haunted past and has the six-digit combination **955214** circled in the text.
- **Crack the safe:** Return to the ground floor, interact with the safe, and enter **955214** manually to claim your cash, rare watch, and trophy.

## Pro Tips & What to Watch Out For

- **Six digits, manual entry:** Unlike many standard GTA 6 safes, this lock requires six digits and may not display the usual on-screen code reminder. Enter **955214** carefully, and check your own rooftop Old Note just in case your save generates a different code.
- **No combat required:** Even though the whispers, mannequins, and sudden darkness scream survival horror, there are no physical ghosts or monsters waiting to ambush you. This mystery is purely atmospheric and unrelated to the Port Gellhorn serial killer questline.
- **Clearing the fog:** The eerie darkness and Silent Hill fog stick around until you unlock the safe and leave the immediate neighborhood. If the weather refuses to reset, swap to your other protagonist elsewhere in Leonida to restore normal skies.
- **Trophy overlap warning:** If you already unlocked **The Truth Is Out There** through GTA 6's UFO encounter, you will not earn it a second time here. Even so, the exclusive Crowex watch, cash payout, and Purple Ratface card make the detour well worth your time.
