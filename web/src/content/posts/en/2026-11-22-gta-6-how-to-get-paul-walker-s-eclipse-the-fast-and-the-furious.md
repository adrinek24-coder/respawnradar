Paul Walker's iconic lime-green Mitsubishi Eclipse from *The Fast and the Furious* is hidden in GTA 6 as the **Maibatsu '97 Penumbra FF**, parked inside a villa garage on **Vespucci Island** (Rialto Islands, just west of Vice Beach). You can steal it the second free roam opens up after the intro, or hold off until early Chapter 2 to register it as your permanent ride for **$0** instead of $6,750.

## Where to Find and Steal the '97 Penumbra FF

Head to the middle of the oval-shaped Vespucci Island on **Neptune Circle** and look for a white villa tucked behind tall privacy hedges right across from a house under construction. Hitting it at night keeps witnesses away and lowers your odds of catching a wanted level.

- **Locate the villa:** Cross west from Vice Beach into the Rialto Islands onto Vespucci Island and spot the white garage door with a narrow side door right next to it.
- **Get onto the property:** Hop the privacy hedge/fence or nudge the driveway gate open with your car.
- **Breach the garage:** Force open the side door next to the main garage. If you've already reached Chapter 3 and own the **Snap Gun**, you can pop the lock silently without setting off any alarms.
- **Kill the security alarm:** Immediately disarm the alarm panel on the wall to your left as you step inside so the cops aren't called.
- **Open the main garage door:** Hit the switch to raise the main garage door before getting in so you don't trash the car on the way out.
- **Boost the car:** Use a **Slim Jim** (unlocked from Moses at the start of Chapter 2) to unlock the door quietly, or smash the window and wait a few seconds for the car alarm to die down. Hotwire it with the L and R sticks and roll out.

## How to Keep It Permanently (Free Registration & Paint Fix)

Stealing the car doesn't lock it into your garage—if you abandon it or wreck it before registering, you're out of luck. Once registered, you can summon it anytime through the **Scooter Bros** app.

- **Free method (Best):** Progress to Chapter 2 until the **Moses** mission (Watkins Auto Parts in Port Gellhorn, Kelly County) unlocks. Moses gives you a one-time free vehicle registration—boost the Penumbra FF first and drive it straight to him to skip the $6,750 fee.
- **Paid method:** Already burned Moses' freebie? Drive the Penumbra FF to Moses' garage (Chapter 2+) or any **Pay 'n' Spray** (Chapter 3+) and pay **$6,750** to register it.
- **Fix the missing green livery:** Registering through Moses forces a respray that strips the movie paint job. Don't panic—head straight to the **Vice Vinyl** mod shop in Vice City and pay **$185** to restore the original paint job. If the car took damage on the drive over, you'll pay **$355** for repairs first (**$540** total).

## Pro Tips & Missable Warnings

- **Take screenshots before registering:** The car spawns with a custom **"HAD YOU"** vanity plate referencing Brian's first race against Dom. Registering it permanently swaps the plate, so get your pics beforehand.
- **Check your garage slots first:** Make sure you actually have an open vehicle storage slot before hauling it to Moses or Pay 'n' Spray.
- **Stick to paved roads:** With 1.9 off-road handling and 3.0 strength, it spins out fast on dirt and crumples easily in heavy crashes.
- **Watch out for the trunk Easter egg:** Crack open the trunk ("boot") and an aggressive snake jumps out at you—Rockstar's cheeky *Toy Story* nod to Woody's "There's a snake in my boot!" line.
