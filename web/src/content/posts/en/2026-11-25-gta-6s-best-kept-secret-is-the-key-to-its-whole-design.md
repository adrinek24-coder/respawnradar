## The Prologue Twist That Anchors GTA 6's Entire Design

Prologue and Chapter 1 spoiler warning: the huge secret Rockstar kept under wraps until GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 is that Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos have a one-year-old son named Tomás. Instead of another rags-to-kingpin fantasy, Lucia turns to crime purely to survive after her RydeMe driver account gets suspended—she even empties every bullet from her pistol before sticking up a loan store while leaving Tomás in a nearby cafe, only to get busted when dye packs explode in the cash bag. With Tomás placed under Lucia's aunt Lorena's care in Liberty State and Lucia stuck on nine months of electronic monitoring, that grounded family stake directly explains why GTA 6 leans so hard into everyday realism: DIY house repairs, a two-way Relationship meter, the Criminal Profile system, and multi-step car theft security.

## Chapter 1 Checklist: Don't Miss These Early Tasks Before Lucia's Release

Because Jason and Lucia were together for less than three months before Tomás was conceived and split before he was born, they are essentially starting over when she gets out of prison. Nail these Chapter 1 steps before picking her up so you start on the front foot:

- **Repair Jason's Key Lento house (permanently missable):** Once free roam opens after Gone Fishing, check the map for four free repair jobs around Brian Heder's stilt house—all planks, shingles, drywall, and paint are already on-site.
- **Knock out steps, roof, and bedroom wall:** Fix the rotten stairs next to the basketball hoop, climb the yellow front-porch ladder to nail new roof shingles, then install the bedroom drywall and paint it Tropical Green, Stormy Blue, or Pastel Peach.
- **Grab the easily missed back-deck railing:** Walk through the house out the back door down to the outdoor gym beside the bench press and look up at the gap in the railing—the interact prompt only shows up from below on the gym side.
- **Complete all Lucia's Release prep tasks:** Repair Jason's car at the mod shop, pick up Lucia's clothes from Andy, buy her necklace at The Siren's Chest, grab flowers and groceries, and get a fresh haircut. Doing this starts you at Crazy in Love (3/4) instead of tier 2.
- **Stash cash under the floorboards:** Jason has no bank account early on and caps out at $2,000 in Pocket Cash, so interact with the loose floorboard beside his bedroom bed to safely stash up to $20,000.

## Honest Pro Tips & Easy Mistakes to Avoid

- **Don't let one protagonist do all the relationship work:** If you grind affection only as Jason and barely switch to Lucia (hold Right on D-pad + R1/RB), their bond can still slip. Switch regularly and return texts and invitations from both sides.
- **Watch for the Criminal Profile breaking point:** Playing clean for Tomás pays off—use Focus Mode's yellow incapacitate targets instead of red lethal shots to climb from Hardened to Professional. If you go on a rampage and see the bottom-right Psycho icon start cracking in slow motion, stop immediately; after three cracks it shatters into Irredeemable permanently.
- **Outrun Advanced Trackers by staying fast:** A red tracker icon on WAiNK notifies the cops and pings your location, but only when you slow down. Keep your foot planted until you ditch the ride or unlock Advanced Tracker removal via Randy's Vehicle Wishlist in Chapter 3.
- **Feed Jason's snake daily:** Interacting with the tank in Jason's living room once per day gives a free Focus boost that helps a ton during early gunfights.
- **Ditch three-star heat on foot:** Cops track your outfit, visible weapons, and vehicle. Keep a spare outfit in your personal car's trunk, swap clothes out of sight, and cut into alleys on foot instead of trying to outdrive Vice City traffic.
