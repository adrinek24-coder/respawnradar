## How to Set Up GTA 6 Remote & Cloud Play

- **PlayStation Portal Cloud Streaming (No PS5 Needed):** Own a digital copy of GTA 6 and an active PS Plus Premium membership. Open the **Cloud Streaming** tab on the Portal home screen, pick **GTA VI** from your eligible library, and hit **Stream**. Bonus: you skip the massive 212 GB local PS5 install.
- **PS5 Remote Play (Portal, PC, or Mobile — No PS Plus Premium Needed):** On your PS5, go to **Settings > System > Remote Play** and switch it on. To wake the console remotely while traveling, open **Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode** and enable both **Stay Connected to the Internet** and **Enable Turning on PS5 from Network**.
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming:** Own the game digitally to stream on Xbox Series X|S or Xbox One, One S, and One X. Keep in mind that Xbox Cloud Gaming streams the **Xbox Series S** visual profile (720p upscaled to ~1080p targeting 30 fps) and enforces session time limits.
- **Emergency Manual Save Before Dropping the Controller:** Finish your active mission and clear any wanted level. Press **Options** (PS5) or **Menu** (Xbox Series X|S), go to **Game > Save Game**, and save to one of the **15 manual slots**.
- **Fix Stuck Xbox Quick Resume Sessions:** If GTA 6 acts weird after suspending for hours, highlight GTA VI on the Xbox dashboard, press **Menu > Manage game and add-ons > Quick Resume**, and toggle it off to force a clean boot.

## Pro Tips & Missable Warnings

- **Do not nuke your only autosave:** GTA 6 has **no mission replay or chapter select at launch**. Loading an older manual file overwrites your single autosave slot, so always save your current run to an empty manual slot before jumping back to replay a mission or branch.
- **Counteract cloud input lag in gunfights:** Players streaming GTA 6 on PlayStation Portal notice input lag most during shootouts. Head to **Options/Menu > Settings > Gameplay**, scroll to the bottom, and enable **Accessible Combat** and **Reduced Vehicle Damage** for stress-free portable play.
- **Turn on Dark Flashbangs for late-night sessions:** In the **Display** tab, switch flashbangs from **Bright** to **Dark** so getting flashed blacks out the screen instead of blinding everyone in the room. While there, drop **Motion Blur** to 0 or 1, turn **Camera Shake** off, and lower **Camera Bob** from 10 to 3–5 to keep the locked 30 fps looking sharp.
- **Test Remote Play before leaving home:** Connect your Portal to your PS5 on home Wi-Fi first. If you forget the Rest Mode network toggles before traveling, nobody at home can save your session.
