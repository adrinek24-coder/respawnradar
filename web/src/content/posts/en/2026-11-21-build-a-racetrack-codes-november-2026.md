You do not need code handouts to scale your plot fast if you play the early loop smart:

- **Finish a complete lap first:** Connect your turns, straights, loops, and jumps into one clean, driveable circuit before buying flashy extras.

- **Prioritize racecar upgrades:** Better racecars bump up your cash-per-lap payout way faster than endlessly hoarding basic track pieces.
- **Protect your offline earnings:** Your track keeps making money while you are offline, so never log out mid-rebuild with a broken, undriveable circuit.
