Moses asks for 7 stolen vehicles in GTA 6, and every single one goes back to Watkins Auto Parts (MW icon) in Port Gellhorn, Kelly County. The chain kicks off in Chapter 2 with the mandatory story mission 'Daily Driver' ('82 Canis Crawler), followed by 6 optional SMS targets worth $18,610 in base payouts plus condition bonuses and the unique Moses' Sultan street racer at the end.

## All 7 Moses Vehicle Locations & Fastest Grab Strats

After 'Daily Driver', Moses texts you targets sequentially as you complete earlier drop-offs. Track what is left anytime via Map > Robberies > Moses Drop-Offs.

- **'82 Canis Crawler (Daily Driver):** White jeep parked inside the old Traders' Union compound on Gellhorn Ave, Port Gellhorn. Skip the front gate guards by sneaking in from the north side near the water tower or hopping the blue fence to the right of the main entrance.
- **Declasse Yosemite 1500:** Bastion St on the east side of the search zone in southwest Port Gellhorn. It is already hooked to a tow truck in a driveway—talking gets you nowhere, so melee the tow driver, release the hook at the back of the truck, and peel out.
- **Karin Futo:** Inside the big Postal building lockup at the southern tip of Draper Island Docks, northwest of Port Gellhorn. Workers call security on sight, so park before the road gate, hop the fence by the train tracks, and enter through the north door or climb the side ladder to drop through the roof hatch. Drop the lone worker, hit the garage switch, and bolt.
- **Annis Hellion:** Parked in a bungalow driveway on Orchard Avenue, just south of Moses' shop in Port Gellhorn. Toss the football in the yard to distract the guard dog (or interact to quiet it), pop the lock with your Slim Jim, hotwire, and head straight north.
- **BF Bandito:** Dirt loop in Buckskin Swamp, Kelly County. Look for the silver off-road buggy parked by the trees or cruising the loop with other off-roaders. Talk to the driver to force them out of the seat, or jump in when parked and win the Struggle prompt if locals grab you.
- **Declasse Vamos:** Rural house on Rockledge Road, northeast of Grayson in Lummox County. Armed rednecks shoot at a trailer before heading to the back deck—sneak past or clear them out. The red Vamos is locked in the east shed behind a keypad you cannot pick: punch in **2727** on the side door, hit the inside garage button, and expect locals chasing you on the road back.
- **Western Nightblade:** Final Chapter motorcycle club compound in Ambrosia. The orange bike sits on the lower floor of the central garage. Load up on ammo beforehand—you can climb the green container on the north side to slip in, but getting spotted triggers a heavy biker shootout.

## Pro Tips & Claiming Moses' Sultan

- **Stop smashing windows:** Always use the Slim Jim Moses gives you in his intro mission. Broken glass and body dents immediately shave cash off your condition bonus.
- **Lose the cops first:** Moses will not open his garage door while you have an active police chase. Drive clean straight to Watkins Auto Parts instead of taking detours.
- **Required for 100%:** Finishing all deliveries triggers 'Collect Moses' Gift', a Strangers & Freaks side mission required for 100% completion that either Jason or Lucia can finish. Open the smaller side garage next to Moses' main bay to grab **Moses' Sultan**.
- **Register it, do not sell it:** You can sell Moses' Sultan for $3,800 or pay $1,600 to register it permanently. Keep it—$3,800 is pocket change later on, and this custom Street Racer is a one-off reward.
