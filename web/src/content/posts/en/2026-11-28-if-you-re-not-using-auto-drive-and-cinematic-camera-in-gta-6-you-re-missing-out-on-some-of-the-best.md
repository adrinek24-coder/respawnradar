To turn on Auto Drive and Cinematic Camera in GTA 6, set a waypoint on your map, get onto a road in any vehicle, hold **Down on the D-Pad**, and press **Options** (PS5) or **Menu** (Xbox). Once the prompt pops up in the bottom-right corner, hold **Circle** (PS5) or **B** (Xbox) and Jason or Lucia will take over the wheel completely. Rockstar never explains this in a tutorial, even though you can use it right from the start of the game.

## How to Set Up and Run Auto Drive

If you hate getting blocked from engaging autopilot whenever you pull off a highway, tweak one gameplay setting before your next cross-county cruise:

- Hop into any land vehicle or boat—Auto Drive works on watercraft too as long as your route stays on navigable water.
- Drop a destination marker on the map, or hold **Down on the D-Pad** to pick a spot through the quick navigation menu.
- Optional fix: head to **Pause Menu > Settings > Gameplay > Auto Drive** and switch from **Restricted** to **Unrestricted** if you want to trigger it off-road or in riskier situations.
- While driving, hold **Down on the D-Pad** and tap **Options / Menu** to enter Cinematic Camera.
- Hold **Circle / B** to lock in Auto Drive, then tap or hold **R2 / RT** (faster) and **L2 / LT** (slower) to cycle through the three speed levels.
- Hold **Circle / B** again to grab manual steering back, or hold **Down on the D-Pad** to exit Cinematic Camera altogether.

## GTA 6 Auto Drive and Cinematic Camera Controls

| Action / Setting | PS5 | Xbox Series X/S | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enter Cinematic Camera | Hold D-Pad Down + Options | Hold D-Pad Down + Menu | Works in vehicles and even during bar hangouts |
| Enable / Disable Auto Drive | Hold Circle | Hold B | Requires an active map waypoint |
| Adjust Speed (Levels 1–3) | L2 (slower) / R2 (faster) | LT (slower) / RT (faster) | Defaults to Speed 2; obeys all traffic rules |
| Switch Camera Angle | Click R3 | Click RS | Cycles exterior, roadside, and cabin views |
| Pan / Adjust Framing | Move Right Stick | Move Right Stick | Nudges the camera without interrupting autopilot |
| Exit Cinematic Camera | Hold D-Pad Down | Hold D-Pad Down | Snaps straight back to standard driving view |
| Restricted Mode (Default) | Settings > Gameplay | Settings > Gameplay | Only available on public roads and safe scenarios |
| Unrestricted Mode | Settings > Gameplay | Settings > Gameplay | Works anywhere, including risky spots with collision risk |

## Pro Tips and Missable Warnings

Using Auto Drive right after big story jobs is hands-down the best way to catch Jason and Lucia's full in-car dialogue. Cinematic Camera cuts straight inside the cabin so you actually see their facial animations, little jokes, and reactions to radio tracks instead of cutting their lines short by slamming into maniac Leonida motorists. You can even flip on Cinematic Camera while hanging out together at bars.

- **Do not treat it as fast travel:** Even on Speed 3, your driver stops at every red light and sits patiently in traffic jams. If you are in a hurry, drive yourself or call a car on the RydeMe app and pay $100 to skip the trip.
- **Watch out for road-rage NPCs:** Auto Drive is not collision-proof, especially on Unrestricted—reckless Leonida drivers can still sideswipe you or hop out to attack your car mid-cruise.
- **Easy to miss stranger events:** Going fully AFK on a 15-minute cross-map cruise means you will likely drive straight past dynamic ambient roadside encounters.
- **Reroute on the fly:** You do not need to cancel Auto Drive to change destinations—just move your waypoint on the pause map and your character updates the route automatically.
