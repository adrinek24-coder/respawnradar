No, you can't just click a spot on the map and warp there in GTA 6. To actually fast travel across Leonida, you have to book a ride with **Paradise Taxi** or the **RydeMe app**, hop in, and pay a flat **$100 skip fee** on top of the base fare. If you don't want to torch your cash every trip, you can also skip rides on buses and trains, turn on hands-free **Autodrive**, abuse free character switching between Jason and Lucia, or snag a free chopper or plane early.

## Instant Fast Travel: Paradise Taxi, RydeMe & Public Transit

All true fast travel in GTA 6 happens in-world once your butt is in a passenger seat. Here is how each option works:

- **Paradise Taxi (Early Chapter 1):** Unlocks right after *Gone Fishing*. Press **D-pad Up** to open your phone, go to **Contacts > All Contacts > Paradise Taxi**, and call a cab. Wait for the white car icon on your minimap, hop in, drop a destination on the map, and confirm the fare. Once rolling, hold **L2 (PS5) / LT (Xbox)** to focus on the driver and press **Triangle / Y** to skip the trip for **$100**. If you're cheap, pay **$5** (up to twice) to make the driver speed up and weave through traffic instead.
- **RydeMe App (Chapter 1, after *Buck Me*):** Unlocks once your BuckMe bank account is live. Open your phone, launch **RydeMe**, pick your safehouse, active waypoint, or a new map pin, and confirm the upfront price. Get in, hold **L2 / LT**, and press **Triangle / Y** to pay the **$100** skip fee. Unlocking **BuckMe Premium** in Chapter 2 also knocks 20% off regular RydeMe fares.
- **Buses & Am Loco Trains (Available Immediately):** Hold **D-pad Down** to reveal nearby bus stops and train stations on the minimap. Check the timetable, hop aboard, pick a stop along the route, and hold **Triangle (PS5) / Y (Xbox)** to skip straight there. Way cheaper than cabs—and completely free if you have GTA+.

## How to Travel Fast (or Hands-Free) Without Paying $100

Want to cover huge chunks of Leonida without bleeding cash? Use these three methods:

- **Autodrive (Cinematic Autopilot):** Drop a custom waypoint (**X / A**) on the map. While driving a car or boat, hold **D-pad Down** and press **Options (PS5) / Menu (Xbox)** for Cinematic Mode, then hold **Circle (PS5) / B (Xbox)** to turn on Autodrive. Tap **L2 / LT** and **R2 / RT** to toggle between its three speed levels. Do yourself a favor first: go to **Settings > Gameplay > Auto Drive** and set it to **Unrestricted** so it works off paved roads too.
- **Partner Switching & Co-Driver (Chapter 2):** Once free switching opens in Chapter 2, hold **D-pad Right** and hit **R1 (PS5) / RB (Xbox)** to jump straight to Jason or Lucia for free—handy when your partner is already hanging out near another district. Riding together? Swap to the passenger seat so the AI drives, then hold **D-pad Right** and press **Square (PS5) / X (Xbox)** to tell them to floor it.
- **Grab an Early Free Plane or Helicopter:** Flying beats every highway in Leonida. Early on, head to **Southwind**—there's a small airstrip and helipad with both a plane and a chopper you can take without instant cop heat. Just steer clear of the neighboring air station's airspace unless you want immediate wanted stars.

## Pro Tips & Easy Mistakes to Avoid

- **Pocket Cash vs. Bank Balance:** Paradise Taxi only takes **pocket cash**, while RydeMe pulls strictly from your **BuckMe bank account**. If a cab won't let you confirm or skip, hit an ATM or switch services.
- **Don't spam skips early on:** That flat $100 skip stacks on top of distance fares—cross-map rides easily hit $300–$400+. Save skips for cross-region hauls, especially before Chapter 3 when money gets steadier.
- **Missable world encounters:** Skipping every drive early means you'll blow past tons of ambient street events and stranger missions scattered across Leonida. Drive manually when exploring new districts.
- **Autodrive burns fuel and obeys red lights:** The AI stops for traffic signals and still eats gas. Don't walk away for a snack on a nearly empty tank—and remember Autodrive doesn't work in planes or helicopters.
- **Quick way to pass time:** Waiting for a time-specific event far from your safehouse bed? Skipping a long taxi or RydeMe ride also advances the in-game clock fast.
