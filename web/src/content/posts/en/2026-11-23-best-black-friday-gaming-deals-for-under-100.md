## Pro Tips & Easy Ways to Mess This Up

- **Use DisplayPort on the AOC 27G50F2:** Its ports are DisplayPort 1.4 and HDMI 1.4. Plugging in over HDMI 1.4 caps your refresh rate below 180Hz on PC. Also keep in mind 1080p stretched across 27 inches looks softer for desktop reading than 24-inch 1080p or 27-inch 1440p.
- **Watch Out for Third-Party Marketplace Resellers:** When Best Buy or Amazon sells out of direct stock, third-party listings snap prices right back up—for example, marketplace sellers already list that same AOC 27G50F2 at $149.99. Always check the seller line before hitting checkout.
- **Don't Wait Past November 30 on PSN:** PlayStation Store Black Friday pricing wraps up on November 30, 2026, and Sony's store can display dynamic pricing across accounts, so verify the cart total before buying.
- **Battery Reality Check:** Both the Razer Naga V2 HyperSpeed and Logitech G305 run on AA batteries rather than internal USB-C charging. Toss in a lightweight lithium AA or rechargeable AAA-with-adapter to cut weight and keep them running for months.
