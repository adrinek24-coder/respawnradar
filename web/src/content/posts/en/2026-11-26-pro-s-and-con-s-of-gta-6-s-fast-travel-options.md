GTA 6 skips the usual free click-to-teleport map menu: every true instant fast-travel option goes through in-world rides like Paradise Taxi, the RydeMe app, or Leonida's buses and trains. Each method trades speed, cash, or drop-off freedom for something else, so picking the right one saves you a ton of money and dead time.

## Every Fast Travel Option: Controls, Pros & Cons

- **Paradise Taxi (Earliest Option):** Unlocks right after the first Chapter 1 mission, Gone Fishing. Pull up your phone (D-pad Up), go to Contacts, All Contacts, and call Paradise Taxi. Drop a waypoint—even in fogged, unexplored areas if a road reaches it—hop in at the white car icon, hold L2 (PS5) or LT (Xbox), and hit Triangle or Y to skip instantly. **Pro:** Available almost from minute one and arrives fast. **Con:** Doesn't show your fare until you get in, and it strictly takes Pocket Cash.
- **RydeMe App (In-Game Rideshare):** Unlocks after completing the Chapter 1 mission Buck Me to set up your BuckMe online bank account. Open your phone (D-pad Up), launch RydeMe, pick your safehouse, map marker, or new destination, choose Standard or Premium, and once inside hold L2/LT plus Triangle/Y to skip. **Pro:** Shows upfront pricing before booking, pulls directly from your BuckMe balance, runs slightly cheaper than cabs, and gets a 20% discount once you unlock BuckMe Premium. **Con:** Drawing a weapon in the car or committing a crime nearby suspends your account for up to 20 minutes, and bookings cancel if your pickup or target is off-road in swamps or remote islands.
- **Buses and Trains (Public Transit):** Hold D-pad Down to highlight nearby stops and stations on your minimap. Head to the stop, check the timetable, wait for the bus or train, board it, select a stop on its route, and hold Triangle/Y to skip. **Pro:** Costs a fraction of a taxi or RydeMe fare. **Con:** Walking to a stop and waiting on schedules kills the whole point of fast travel, and fixed routes often drop you miles from where you actually want to be.
- **Free Workarounds (Character Switch, Auto Drive & Aircraft):** Once both Jason and Lucia are playable, switching between them is free and instant—great for snapping back near the safehouse, though you can't pick the destination. When riding together, hold D-pad Right + R1/RB to hop into the passenger seat and let your partner drive (D-pad Right + Square/X tells them to hurry). Solo Auto Drive (hold D-pad Down, press Options/Menu for cinematic mode, hold Circle/B) is free, too, but plays out in real time while obeying traffic lights. Need to cross the map fast and hit remote keys or unfog everything? Steal a plane, helicopter, or military jet—just expect wanted heat on the jet.

## Pro Tips & Easy Mistakes to Avoid

- **Don't waste $100 skips on short hops:** The $100 skip surcharge stays flat regardless of distance. Early in Chapter 1 and 2 when money is tight, pay $5 (up to twice via L2/LT + Square/X) to make your driver floor it instead—just watch out, because on the second $5 bump they start plowing through traffic.
- **Bypass the RydeMe 20-minute ban instantly:** Accidentally pulled a gun inside a RydeMe or triggered police heat next to the car? Save your game and reload right away to clear the suspension without waiting.
- **Watch the in-game clock:** Skipping a ride does not freeze time; the clock advances as if you drove the whole way. Use it to fast-forward hours on purpose, not when racing a time-sensitive trigger.
- **Keep both wallets topped up:** Paradise Taxi refuses bank money and strictly pulls Pocket Cash, while RydeMe only bills your BuckMe account. Carry a bit of cash so you aren't stuck hunting for an ATM.
- **Ride along once in a while:** Skipping every single trip means missing out on quirky driver personalities and dialogue—plus you can scroll your in-game phone during real-time rides.
