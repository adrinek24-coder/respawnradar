The Kingpin Bowling safe code in GTA 6 is **0300**—a clean nod to bowling a perfect 300 game. You do not need to hunt down the clue first; if you are already standing in front of the big green safe in the back office, just punch in **0300** (including the leading zero) and grab the cash.

## How to Find the Safe Inside Kingpin Bowling

Kingpin Bowling is a Kolt & Associates Illegal Operation on US-9 in southern Port Gellhorn, Kelly County, right south of the Pawn & Gun Ammu-Nation. It pops up on your map in Chapter 2 as **Hit Kingpin Bowling** right after the *Coffee Break* main mission.

- Fight your way through the alley toward the back on the far east side of the building.
- Look for the counter next to the **Staff Only** door and the **CHILLED** fridge.
- Go through the **Storage** door directly to the left of that counter.
- Open the wooden **Office** door on your left to find the large green safe in the north-east corner.
- Enter **0300** for a quiet open, or blow the door off with a Sticky Bomb. Expect roughly $3,400 to $3,840 in cash inside.

## Where the Code Clue Actually Hides

If you tore the bowling alley apart looking for a note, Rockstar pulled a fast one—the code is not even in the building. The notepad on the table in front of the safe points you toward **Brooks Boatworks**, another Kolt & Associates operation in northern Port Gellhorn.

- Head to Brooks Boatworks and move to the east side of the compound, just right of the main warehouse.
- Find the small gray building with stairs and a white door.
- Go upstairs and inspect the wall map hanging above the weapon case (next to the whiteboard).
- Zooming in gives you all three fixed Port Gellhorn gang safe codes in one stop.

## Pro Tips & Easy-to-Miss Details

- **Do not drop the leading zero:** Typing `300` fails—you have to input all four digits as **0300**.
- **Free Sticky Bombs on site:** If you prefer blowing the safe and have not unlocked Sticky Bombs at Ammu-Nation yet (unlocked in Chapter 3's *Credit Checked*), check the side hallway inside Kingpin Bowling for a toolbox loaded with Sticky Bombs and grenades.
- **Bring your partner for extra bag space:** Hold D-Pad Right to call your partner along. Besides watching your back, you can hand off full loot bags to them so you can clear out the pill packages on the center tables too, then fence everything at Dunk's Trailer.
- **Let's Go Bowling trophy delay:** Grabbing all three Key Valuables (Watch, Gutterball Moreland 850 shotgun, and the Safe) unlocks the Bronze **Let's Go Bowling** trophy/achievement, but it only pops shortly after you escape the 2-star wanted level. Missed something? Do not stress—the hideout respawns after a few in-game weeks, so it is not permanently missable.
