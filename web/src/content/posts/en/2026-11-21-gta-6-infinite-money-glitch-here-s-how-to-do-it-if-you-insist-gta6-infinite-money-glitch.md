## Quick Answer / TL;DR

Following GTA 6's November 19, 2026 launch, players quickly uncovered a repeatable infinite money glitch in Story Mode. The fastest early-game method uses a red barn in South Vice-Dale County (north of Key Lento, east of Hamlet): loot the cocaine and cash into stash bags, store them in your personal vehicle's trunk, create a manual save, and reload it immediately. Your vehicle keeps the bags while the barn's loot respawns, earning roughly $11,280 on your first run and around $8,000 to $10,000 per repeat.

## Step-by-Step: The Red Barn Stash Bag Glitch

Before heading out, make sure you are driving your personal vehicle—stolen cars will not retain your stash bags in the trunk—and unlock your first fence if you want to cash out right away.

- Drive your personal vehicle north of Key Lento into South Vice-Dale County, east of Hamlet, and stop at the isolated red barn.
- Head inside to the back office and grab all the cocaine from the table, shelves, and office safe, packing it into your first stash bag.
- Carry the full stash bag outside and place it into your personal vehicle's trunk.
- Return inside to the open van in the center of the barn to collect a second stash bag filled with cash, then top off the remaining space with cocaine from the nearby vans (including the teddy bear art van and the van parked outside).
- Place the second bag in your trunk—you can accumulate up to four stash bags inside your vehicle.
- Open the Pause Menu, navigate to Game > Save Game, and create a manual save in a separate slot (do not rely on autosaves).
- Immediately reload that manual save. You will respawn at Jason's house with your personal car and all collected stash bags still safely in the trunk.
- Drive back to the red barn where the cash and cocaine should have respawned, then repeat the loop.

## How to Cash Out & Alternative Store Reset Glitch

To convert stolen goods into spendable cash, finish the Chapter 1 mission 'Lab Fees (Collect Brian's Rent)' to unlock Brian's Lockup (marked with a green icon in southernmost Key Lento). Enter keypad code 7700, drop off your stash bags inside, wait a few in-game days, and collect your payout from the mailbox outside Jason's house. If you prefer another early exploit, try the store robbery reset: rob the burger restaurant, gas station, and convenience stores along the beach road near Jason's house in Key Lento, lose your wanted level, start any story mission with an opening cutscene, select 'Restart Mission' during the cutscene, and then select 'Quit Mission' once it restarts. Returning to free roam immediately resets the closed store timers.

## Pro Tips and What to Watch Out For

- Spoiler-free Chapter 1 warning: Most guides recommend waiting until Chapter 2 to farm heavily—if you do farm in Chapter 1, spend your accumulated cash before starting the mission 'Tourist Trap'.
- Maximize fence payouts: Brian's Lockup offers a Very Low payout rate for narcotics in Chapter 1, whereas Donnie's Trailer in northern Key Lento pays slightly better (Low) for narcotics in Chapter 1, and Chapter 3 fences offer High payouts.
- Fixing respawn hiccups: If the barn loot doesn't immediately reappear, save and reload once or twice more or let a couple of in-game days pass.
- Protect your earnings: Store extra cash in your safe or deposit it into your BuckMe account once unlocked.
- Consider your immersion: As many players and IGN have pointed out, breaking GTA 6's economy early can take the thrill out of Jason and Lucia's carefully paced progression—use it only if you genuinely want to skip the early grind.
