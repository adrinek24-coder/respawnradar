GTA 6 players traced Chase's fox suit from the viral Operation Furball encounter directly to real-life fursuiter FoxiFelix—both suits share nearly identical white shooting-star markings with three trailing stripes on each knee, and bodysuit maker YCHsuits claimed Rockstar used one of its designs for the storyline. In-game, your call is simple: smash through the nighttime police blockade and rescue Chase, because walking away permanently locks you out of the Furverts' compound and its top-tier reward.

## How to Trigger and Complete Operation Furball

In Leonida, Governor Mike Jimenez officially outlawed being a furry, backed by an Anti-Furry Association TV ad declaring open hunting season and flagging slang like "uwu," "rawr xD," and "hewwo". That crackdown sets up a live free-roam standoff that players nicknamed Operation Furball after a throwaway line at the end of the quest.

- Cruise around Vice City at night and watch for red-and-blue VCPD lights under a helicopter spotlight—one verified run triggered right after rolling out of a Pay 'n' Spray.
- Pull up to the roadside blockade where cops have Chase cornered in his fox suit, break through, and let him hop into your ride.
- Ditch your instant wanted level by breaking line of sight—ducking into a covered parking garage works great to shake the chopper.
- Link up with a second white-suited furry inside the garage, then escort both of them to the Furverts' secret compound on an affluent island.
- Claim your choice of cash or the custom colorful SMG at the compound, and listen closely as the duo casually drop the phrase "Operation Furball" right before you leave.

## Save vs. Kill Chase: Consequences and Rewards

| Outcome / Factor | Save Chase (Best Pick) | Kill or Ignore Chase |

| Wanted Level | Immediate VCPD chase with helicopter spotlight | Zero wanted level since hunting furries is legal in Leonida |
| Furverts Compound | Unlocked with full escort sequence | Permanently locked out |
| Main Reward | Choice of custom SMG or cash ($15,000 reported by Dexerto; $20,000 by GamesBeat) | Loose street pocket change only |
| Extra Lore | Underground Furverts dialogue and Operation Furball tease | Misses all follow-up dialogue |

## Real-Life Fursuit Clues and SMG Art Claims

Once clips of Chase went viral, eagle-eyed players matched his leg markings to FoxiFelix's real suit: matching white shooting stars on both knees with three trailing stripes, plus a similar tail and a habit of meowing in the van that Felix recognized immediately—only the fox head looks different. YCHsuits, credited by Felix for the bodysuit, weighed in saying Rockstar used one of its designs while another maker built the head. Another fursuiter, Chase the Fox, got flooded with DMs over the shared name and fox theme, though the knee stars point specifically to FoxiFelix. On top of that, players on X alleged the custom SMG's wolf decal closely mirrors a 2021 commission by furry artist GabyT. Rockstar has not publicly confirmed the inspiration behind Chase's suit or responded to the SMG artwork claims.

## Pro Tips & Missable Warnings

- **Drop a manual save first:** GTA 6 has no mission replay feature. Pause and make a quick manual save the second you spot the police lights at night if you want to see both outcomes without losing the compound.
- **Grab the custom SMG over cash:** You can easily stack cash later by raiding the 39 Illegal Gang Operations across Leonida, whereas the custom SMG is a unique keepsake from this encounter.
- **No confirmed player fursuit yet:** Don't expect a suit drop at the compound—whether Jason or Lucia can ever unlock a wearable fursuit remains unconfirmed.
