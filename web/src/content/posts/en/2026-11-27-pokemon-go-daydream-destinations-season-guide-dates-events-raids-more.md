Pokémon GO **Daydream Destinations** runs from **Tuesday, December 1, 2026, at 10:00 AM** to **Monday, March 1, 2027, at 10:00 AM local time**. This three-month Alola-focused season headlines **Pokémon GO Tour: Alola**, Raid Day debuts for **Mega Froslass** and **Mega Drampa**, Max Battle Days for **Gigantamax Inteleon** (Shiny debut) and **Gigantamax Toxtricity**, plus a refreshed Egg pool and Mega-enabled GO Battle League.

## Day-One Action Plan & Season Checklist

Follow these steps right at season launch so you don't leave free damage, Stardust, or Candy on the table:

- **Claim GO Pass: November before December 3 (10:00 AM local):** Any unclaimed November pass rewards vanish after that deadline.
- **Hit GO Pass Rank 1 before raiding:** Seasonal perks are tied to monthly GO Pass milestones now—reaching Rank 1 immediately switches on **2× Friendship bonus damage in raids** and extra Friendship XP. **GO Pass: December** runs December 1 to January 5 and includes a Kyurem encounter.
- **Hunt Pyukumuku & Shadow Dialga early:** **Pyukumuku** debuts (with Shiny odds) in 3-star raids and the event GO Pass during *Tropical Paradise* from December 1 to 7. **Shadow Dialga** takes over Legendary Shadow Raids from December 2 to January 5.
- **Prep raid & Max Battle counters now:** Squad up for **Gigantamax Inteleon** in 6-star Max Battles on Saturday, December 5 (2:00–5:00 PM local) for its Shiny debut. Build Ground, Fighting, Fire, and Steel attackers ahead of **Gigantamax Toxtricity**, **Mega Drampa**, and **Mega Froslass**.
- **Grab the free GBL Timed Research Pass:** Once **GO Battle League: Daydream Destinations** launches on December 1 at 1:00 PM PST, claim the free pass from the in-game shop. Page 1 awards PvP starter encounters, and subsequent pages (100 battles / 50 wins each) drop Stardust, Rare Candy XL, an Elite Fast TM, and an Elite Charged TM.
- **Walk 50 km/week for Adventure Sync 10 km Eggs:** **Gible, Drampa, and Frigibax** are in the 10 km Adventure Sync pool—the easiest way to stockpile Candy before Mega Drampa drops.

## Pro Tips & Missable Warnings

- **Pop a Star Piece before opening the Battle screen:** Don't tap the Battle menu after 1:00 PM PST on December 1 without a Star Piece running, or you'll miss 50% extra Stardust on your Twilight Trails end-of-season payout.
- **Don't mix up the March 1 end day:** Several calendars misprinted March 1, 2027 as a Tuesday, but it's actually a **Monday** at 10:00 AM local time—plan your final weekend grind accordingly.
- **GO Battle Thursdays pause for GO Tour Global:** The 10-set, 4× win-Stardust Thursday bonus is suspended during Pokémon GO Tour: Alola – Global week, so farm your Stardust sets earlier in February.
- **Hold your 7th-day Research stamp if you're close:** Because the new Research Breakthrough encounters aren't officially revealed ahead of launch, wait until after 10:00 AM on December 1 to check the live pool before claiming.
