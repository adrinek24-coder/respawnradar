**Quick Answer (TL;DR):** You can steal a **Hauners P-996 Lazer** fighter jet in GTA 6 as early as **Chapter 1**—even before Lucia leaves prison. Head west from Jason's house in Key Lento through Abismo Key and Southwind to **Mile Zero Air Station** on Goose Key in Mariana County. The jets sit unlocked in the northern airfield next to the runways, though boarding one triggers a 4- to 5-star Wanted Level and immediate aerial pursuit.

## Where to Find the Early Fighter Jet in GTA 6

The only military fighter jet spawn on the map is inside **Mile Zero Air Station**, Leonida's heavily fortified air base at the far southwestern tip of **Mariana County**. Follow Sunbelt Highway (Route 1) roughly 2.5 miles southwest from Jason's safehouse in Key Lento across two long bridges to Southwind, then continue onto Goose Key. Multiple **Hauners P-996 Lazer** jets—armed with machine guns and missiles—are parked unlocked around the northern and northeastern runways and hangars, meaning you do not need a Key Cloner to hijack one.

## Step-by-Step: How to Steal the P-996 Lazer in Chapter 1

- **Prep before you go:** Create a manual save, stock up on healing items, and buy body armor at Ammu-Nation. Leave your personal vehicle behind and steal a fast, sturdy street car instead, since it will be destroyed or abandoned inside the base.
- **Route A – Southeast Stunt Jump (Fastest Ground Entry):** Drive to the southeastern edge of Mile Zero Air Station, weave past concrete barriers and patrols on the southern access road, and hit the large dirt mound (**Mile Zero Air Station Southeast** stunt jump) at high speed to vault straight over the barbed-wire fence onto the airfield.
- **Route B – Boat & Armored Car (IGN Chapter 1 Strategy):** Steal a boat at the Southwind docks, circle the island to park near the bridge, and sprint ashore to the two armored military vehicles guarded by soldiers. Hop into the one facing away from you (triggering an instant 5-star Wanted Level), launch straight ahead off the sand dune ramp over the fence, avoid the ditch to your right, skirt the perimeter, and crash through the inner gate.
- **Pick the right runway jet:** Drive straight toward the group of jets in the northeastern section right beside the main runways. Skip any jets facing directly into a building, as you will not have enough clearance to take off safely.
- **Survive startup and lift off:** Climb into the cockpit and use healing items if soldiers light you up—the jet has a noticeable engine startup sequence where you are a sitting duck. Avoid ramming parked jets so they do not explode, gain speed on the tarmac, and pull back on the stick to take off.

## Pro Tips & What to Watch Out For

- **Turn on the fuel gauge:** Fighter jets burn through fuel much faster than civilian planes. Enable the fuel gauge above your minimap in the UI settings so you know when to land or bail out with a $250 Ammu-Nation parachute.
- **Expect relentless pursuit:** Military jets are fitted with an **Advanced Tracker**, allowing the military to constantly track your position and scramble two enemy fighter jets to shoot you down. Stay fast and check for Rhino tanks—parked in the southern half of the base—before boarding, as a tank shell will obliterate your jet during startup.
- **You cannot store the jet:** There is no personal hangar or garage storage for the P-996 Lazer. A spawn cheat for the Hauners P-996 Lazer is reportedly tied to completing every challenge in the game, though the exact code remains unconfirmed.
- **Use a quieter plane to clear map fog:** If your goal is peaceful exploration rather than chaos, grab a free, unguarded **Mammoth Dodo** seaplane from the small hangar and docks in Southwind, or steal the **Bottcher Duster** crop duster from the airstrip off Boundary Road in Mariana County—both let you fly without triggering military retaliation.
