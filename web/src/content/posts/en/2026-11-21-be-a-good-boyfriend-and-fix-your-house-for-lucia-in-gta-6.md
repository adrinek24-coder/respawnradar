## Quick Answer / TL;DR

To fix up Jason Duval's rent-free Key Lento safehouse for Lucia in GTA 6, complete five quick DIY checklist tasks across four spots in Chapter 1 after finishing Gone Fishing: repair the front steps, patch the leaky roof, replace the missing back-deck railing by the outdoor gym, install the bedroom drywall, and paint the bedroom wall. None of the projects cost money, and finishing them before picking Lucia up in Coming Home gives you an early relationship boost and a more romantic welcome cutscene.

## All 5 Jason's House Repair Locations: Step-by-Step

Open your map and select Jason's House in Key Lento to track your repair checklist. Look for the paintbrush interact icons around the property—you can tackle these jobs at any time of day.

- **1. Fix Steps (Front Driveway):** Head to the front exterior staircase next to Jason's parked car, mailbox, driveway, and basketball hoop (avoid the waterside stairs). Approach the stack of wooden 2x4 planks sitting on a blue tarp beside the stairs and hold Triangle (PS5) or Y (Xbox). Pull off the rotten wood with L2 + R2 and tap X (Xbox: LT + RT, then tap A), then fasten the new boards with R2 / RT.
- **2. Fix Leaky Roof (East Porch Ladder):** Walk out to the front porch beside the front door and climb the yellow ladder onto the roof. Head over to the blue tarp next to the missing shingles, interact with the repair spot, and tap R2 / RT to secure the new shingles with the nail gun.
- **3. Fix Railing (Back Deck Outdoor Gym):** This is the easiest repair to miss. Exit through the back sliding glass door onto the back deck, then walk down the stairs into the lower workout area with the BBQ, bench press, and kettlebells. Turn around to face the house—the broken gap sits right beside the silver ladder. Trigger the prompt from the gym side and press R2 / RT to nail the new rails in place.
- **4. Fix Wall (Bedroom Drywall):** Go inside Jason's bedroom behind the living room couch. Opposite the foot of the bed, you will see an unfinished section with exposed wooden framing and stacked wall panels. Interact with the panels and hold R2 (PS5) or RT (Xbox) to screw the drywall into place.
- **5. Paint Wall (Bedroom Paint Cans):** Once the drywall is up (or as soon as Lucia's Release preparations unlock), interact with the paint cans near the foot of the bed. Choose Tropical Green, Stormy Blue, or Pastel Peach, then use the Left Stick to dip the roller in the tray and paint the repaired wall.

## Pro Tips, Missable Warning & Common Mistakes

- **Do not approach the railing from the upper deck:** Trying to trigger the railing repair from the upper back porch outside the sliding door will not work. Always walk down the stairs to the weights and face the house from the gym side.
- **Finish before Coming Home and keep a manual save:** Most playthroughs lock out the original house preparation tasks once you pick Lucia up from Hamlet Correctional in Coming Home. Even though some players report making repairs afterward, completing all five jobs before picking her up guarantees the early heart-plus relationship boosts and the upgraded romantic arrival scene—drop a manual save before driving to the prison just in case.
- **Pick whichever paint color you like:** Tropical Green, Stormy Blue, and Pastel Peach are 100% cosmetic choices. Lucia reacts to Jason putting in the effort to finish the house, not to which shade of paint you rolled on.
- **Paint Wall missing from your checklist?** If the paint prompt does not trigger immediately after screwing in the drywall, advance Chapter 1 through Feng Shui until the Lucia's Release preparations appear on the Story map, then return to the bedroom.
- **Stack extra pre-Lucia preparations:** Once you see the House repairs complete notification, maximize your starting romance rating before Coming Home by grabbing groceries, flowers, a custom necklace, a haircut for Jason, and picking up Lucia's clothes from Andy at Manatee's Rest to store in Jason's trunk.
