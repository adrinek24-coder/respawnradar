You can play Minor Deity right now for free by joining the active Steam Playtest directly from its Steam store page. Following its Origin Point Games Showcase 2026 trailer, the gridless world-building sandbox from solo dev Re: cOg Mission (creator of World Turtles) has no confirmed release date or price yet, but it is slated for PC on Steam and Itch.

## How to Get Into the Steam Playtest Right Now

You do not need to hunt for third-party keys to test the current build. Do this instead:

- Open Steam on Windows PC and pull up the **Minor Deity** store page.
- Scroll below the main trailers to the **Join the Minor Deity Playtest** banner.
- Hit **Join the Playtest** (or **Request Access**) to add the test client straight to your Steam Library.
- Clear at least 8 to 12 GB of drive space before installing.
- Tweak your comfort settings on launch: the game already lists 8 accessibility and QoL options, including **Save Anytime**, **Adjustable Difficulty**, **Camera Comfort**, **Color Alternatives**, **Adjustable Text Size**, and **No QTEs**.

## Core Mechanics: Terraforming, Weather & Trade Routes

Minor Deity drops rigid building grids in favor of massive, fluid landscapes backed by an active weather engine. Here is what actually drives your worlds under the hood:

- **Four-Element Terraforming:** Command Earth, Water, Air, and Fire to build anything from tiny solo islands to sprawling continents. The engine lets you sculpt 9 million underlying terrain points and stamp, rotate, move, or resize entire continents in real time.
- **Dynamic Localized Climate:** Beneath the surface sit 160,000 climate hexes tracking five core stats: **surface water, groundwater, temperature, wind speed, and erosion**. Weather propagates continuously across the map, and terrain visuals shift live as those numbers change.
- **Living Ecosystems:** Vegetation, dense forests, and animal herds do not just sit there as props — you have to dial in local climate conditions so they survive and flourish.
- **Towns & Multi-Route Trade:** Lay out gridless towns for growing populations, drop resource outposts, place roads and fences, and push trade across **road, river, and sea routes**.
- **Progression Goal:** Beyond pure sandbox tinkering, the dev is adding structured objectives aimed at building your reputation in the **Halls of the Minor Deities**.

## PC System Requirements (Minimum vs. Recommended)

Parallel-threaded terrain calculations keep the CPU requirements surprisingly mild, while rendering massive maps and millions of objects hits your GPU hard. Check your rig against the official Steam specs:

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |

## Honest Pro Tips & Early Warnings

- **Know what the playtest actually is:** In an August 2026 dev update, Re: cOg Mission was upfront that the core sandbox is already fun to mess around in, while the traditional game loop and specific objectives are actively being layered on top. Jump in expecting a deep world-sculpting sandbox first, not a finished campaign.
- **Watch that GPU jump:** Minimum specs ask for a GTX 1060, but Recommended shoots straight up to an RTX 3070 or RX 6800 XT. On mid-range or older GPUs, start with smaller island chains instead of maxing out continent-scale tree and herd brushes right away.
- **Sculpt climate before placing towns:** Because wind, erosion, and groundwater actively reshape how habitable an area is, lock down your rivers, elevation, and weather balance before laying out permanent towns and sea trade routes.
- **No Linux or Steam Deck guarantee yet:** Windows is the only confirmed platform right now — Steam Deck status is Unknown and Linux support is still a work in progress.
