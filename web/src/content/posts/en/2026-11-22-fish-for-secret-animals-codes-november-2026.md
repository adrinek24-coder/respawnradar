## How to Redeem Codes in Fish for Secret Animals

Make sure you clear the short intro first before heading to the menu. Once that is done, follow these steps:

- Launch **Fish for Secret Animals** on Roblox and finish the brief tutorial (catch your first few Secret Animals and hatch an egg for your first pet).
- Click the **Settings** gear icon in the top-right corner of your screen.
- Scroll down inside the Settings window to locate the **Enter code** text box.
- Enter or paste the code in **ALL CAPS** — codes are strictly case-sensitive.
- Press **REDEEM** and check your inventory to confirm the items landed.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Stop burning top bait on starter gear:** Rainbow Worms and Jackpot Bait are single-use consumables. Dropping +100 Luck or a Jackpot Cast with your starter rod in the first zone wastes the boost on weak loot pools. Hold them until you upgrade your rod, Power, and Casting stats and unlock higher biomes.
- **Watch for trailing spaces:** Copy-pasting often grabs an invisible space at the end, which makes valid codes fail.
- **Button stuck? Server hop:** Servers cap at 5 players. If clicking REDEEM does nothing, leave and rejoin a fresh server before assuming a code died.
- **Sell early, stash rares later:** Sell common pulls to rush your first rod upgrades, but store rare Secret Animals at your home base so they print passive income while you fish.
- **Where new codes drop:** Chill Shark releases fresh codes around major patches (like Update 3) and like milestones via the official Discord server, the Chill Shark Roblox community, and in-game update notes.
