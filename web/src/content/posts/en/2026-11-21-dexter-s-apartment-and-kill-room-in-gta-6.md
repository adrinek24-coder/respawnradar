Dexter's apartment, rooftop kill kit, and boat are all clustered at **Trinity Apartments** on **Washington Island** (Vice Beach, Vice-Dale County), while his plastic-wrapped kill room sits inside a garage on **Surfside Drive** in southern **Key Lento**. You can hit both locations the second you finish the Prologue and get free-roam control of Jason.

## How to Find Dexter's Apartment, Rooftop Kit, and Boat

From Jason's house in Mariana County, hop on the freeway heading northeast into Vice-Dale County—it's roughly a five-to-six-minute drive. Even before exploring the area, you can zoom into your map to spot neighborhood labels.

- **Locate Trinity Apartments:** Zoom into Vice Beach east of Vice City and set a waypoint near the top of **Washington Island**. Look for the angular waterfront condo with a pool on its north side, sitting right between the Pay 'N' Spray and the Vice Beach Top Fun Drones shop. The *Trinity Apartments* name itself is a direct nod to Season 4's Trinity Killer.
- **Check Dexter's corner unit:** Climb the large spiral staircase to the second floor and walk west along the exterior corridor to the final door. That northwestern corner apartment overlooks the water and sports the exact AC unit outside where Dexter hid his blood slide box in the show.
- **Grab the rooftop kill kit:** Use the ladder on the side of the building to climb onto the roof and walk near the top of the spiral staircase. You'll find a syringe, rope, gloves, tape, handcuffs, a knife, and an orange toolbox. You can pocket the free knife and grab cash from the container (reports put the payout between roughly $68 and $200).
- **Steal the Slice of Life lookalike:** Head back down the spiral stairs to the docking bay on the western side of Trinity Apartments. The first boat in the row is a **Lure Predator L35P** center-console boat modeled closely after Dexter's *Slice of Life*—hop in and take it out to sea.

## How to Find Dexter's Kill Room in Key Lento

The creepiest tribute is actually right next to your starting stomping grounds in Mariana County.

- **Drive to Surfside Drive:** Head to the middle-bottom / southern stretch of **Key Lento** along **Surfside Drive**.
- **Pop open the garage:** Look for an ordinary-looking house with a garage you can open or break into.
- **Check out the setup:** Inside, the walls are draped in plastic sheeting around a central metal surgical table. You'll spot a chainsaw, hammers, cleavers, knives, surgical gear, bloody garbage bags, and a mysterious container paired with a syringe tucked beneath the table.

## Pro Tips & Missable Warnings

- **Don't waste time trying to breach the apartment:** Dexter's condo door won't open and there's no interior behind it, so snap your screenshot by the AC unit and move straight to the side ladder.
- **Stay out of the attached house at Surfside Drive:** Breaking into the main house connected to the Key Lento kill-room garage triggers an alarm. Stick to inspecting the garage unless you want unwanted heat.
- **Watch for witnesses at the dock:** Jacking the Lure Predator behind Trinity Apartments can slap you with a wanted level if nearby NPCs spot you—gun it east into open water to break line of sight fast.
- **Hold off on deep-sea diving for now:** Rumors about Dexter's underwater Gulf Stream dumping ground are making the rounds, but until players verify exact coordinates, don't burn an hour blindly scuba diving off Vice City.
