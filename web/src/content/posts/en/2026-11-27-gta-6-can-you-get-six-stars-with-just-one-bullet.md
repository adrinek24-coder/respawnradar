No, one bullet alone stalls out at five stars—but you **can** hit a six-star wanted level in GTA 6 with **one bullet and one grenade**. The trick abuses the fact that your AI-controlled partner in free-roam is completely invulnerable and carries unlimited ammo. Because GTA 6 refuses to hand out the sixth star while the player sits completely passive, you just let your partner grind you to five stars and toss a single grenade to finish the job.

## How to Pull Off the One-Bullet + One-Grenade Setup

- **Clear the intro chapters first:** You cannot trigger a six-star wanted level during GTA 6's opening tutorial sections, so make sure you have progressed past the intro.
- **Bring both protagonists to Taco Bomb:** Free-roam together as Jason and Lucia and head to **Taco Bomb in Port Gellhorn**. Its tight interior bottlenecks police and gives you solid indoor cover.
- **Fire your single bullet:** Take control of one character (such as Jason), step outside, and shoot a single staff member or bystander to kick off the police response.
- **Hide behind the fridge:** Walk right back inside and tuck behind hard cover like the kitchen fridge—just stay out of the drive-thru window's line of sight so cops outside cannot snipe you.
- **Let your partner clear waves (45–60 minutes):** Your AI partner (e.g., Lucia) will automatically gun down every cop and SWAT officer entering the joint with zero damage taken and infinite ammo. Armored trucks will lock down the exits, choppers will swarm overhead, and your wanted level will climb to **five stars** and freeze there.
- **Drop one grenade for the sixth star:** Once a pile of incapacitated, still-alive cops builds up on the restaurant floor, pull the pin on **one grenade** and toss it into the pile—the blast immediately pops your sixth star.

## Pro Tips & Missable Warnings

- **Drop a manual save beforehand:** Six stars is permanent by normal rules—the game pops a warning that escape is impossible, almost all of Leonida becomes a search zone, and swapping outfits or vehicles won't save you. You only clear it by getting busted, getting wasted, or reloading an earlier save.
- **Protect your Criminal Profile:** Going on a mass-casualty spree tanks a clean "Professional" Criminal Profile, so always do six-star experiments on a throwaway save.
- **Watch the drive-thru window:** Cops love trying to angle shots into Taco Bomb from outside; keep solid kitchen cover between you and the window at all times.
- **Don't let your console sleep:** Waiting 45–60 minutes behind a fridge can trigger your PS5 or Xbox rest-mode timer, so tap a stick every so often.
