**Quick Answer / TL;DR:** **L.A. Noire 2 is not officially confirmed** by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. However, an old 2023 leak just went viral again following the November 19, 2026 release of GTA 6 because players discovered the same anonymous source accurately described GTA 6's ending alongside several pre-launch gameplay systems. According to that unverified post, Rockstar's next major release after GTA 6 could be an L.A. Noire sequel set in **San Francisco**, built around the **Zodiac Killer**, targeting a **2030** release window. Everything below is completely free of GTA 6 ending spoilers.

## Why the 2023 L.A. Noire 2 Leak Is Trending After GTA 6

The rumor originally started as an anonymous 4chan post in 2023 before GTAForums user Shahanshah relayed it. It briefly circulated in September 2026, but players could not verify its story claims until GTA 6 actually launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Here is why the leak is getting renewed scrutiny:

- **Accurate GTA 6 ending details:** Players on the GamingLeaksAndRumours subreddit went back to the 2023 thread after GTA 6's launch and reported that the leaker's ending breakdown matches the finished game.
- **Confirmed gameplay mechanics:** Long before official reveals, the same post correctly outlined Jason and Lucia's relationship mechanic and GTA 6's Criminal Profile system.
- **Early four-protagonist concept:** The post claimed Rockstar originally planned four playable characters before narrowing GTA 6 down to two—something Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson later confirmed, though it is worth noting Axios had already reported the four-protagonist detail a year before the 4chan post.
- **Key caveat:** A strong track record on GTA 6 proves the poster likely had genuine insight in 2023, but early-stage projects inside major studios frequently change scope, shift release windows, or get shelved without official confirmation.

## Every Rumored L.A. Noire 2 Detail and Real-World Studio Clues

Beyond the resurfaced forum post, several real corporate moves at Rockstar and Take-Two keep fans speculating about a return to the detective series. Here is what the leak claims alongside verified industry facts:

- **San Francisco setting:** Rather than returning to 1947 Los Angeles, the rumored sequel moves north to San Francisco.
- **Zodiac Killer investigation:** The narrative reportedly revolves around the real-life unidentified Zodiac Killer who terrorized Northern California in the late 1960s and early 1970s.
- **Next-generation facial scanning:** The source claims Rockstar leadership wants to revive the original game's signature facial-scanning tech and push it dramatically further in hopes of building the best-looking game ever made.
- **2030 target window:** As of 2023, the game was described as being in very early development with a tentative 2030 release target (or no earlier than 2030).
- **The Rockstar Australia factor:** In March 2025, Rockstar acquired Video Games Deluxe—the Sydney studio founded by L.A. Noire director Brendan McNamara that built L.A. Noire: The VR Case Files and updated the GTA Trilogy—and renamed it Rockstar Australia. While fans connect the dots, nothing officially confirms what Rockstar Australia is developing next.
- **Take-Two CEO's April 2026 stance:** Asked directly about L.A. Noire in April 2026, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick emphasized there was nothing to announce and that any reveal would come from Rockstar, while reiterating that teams always evaluate legacy IP when there is passion behind it.

## Pro Tips, Spoiler Warnings, and Common Mistakes to Avoid

If you are digging into this rumor while playing GTA 6, keep these practical warnings in mind:

- **Do not search the original 2023 thread if you have not finished GTA 6:** Looking up the archived Shahanshah GTAForums post or the active GamingLeaksAndRumours discussion will immediately expose unmarked GTA 6 ending spoilers.
- **Treat 2030 as a best-case estimate, not a promise:** The 2030 estimate came from 2023—before GTA 6 slipped from 2025 to May 2026 and finally to November 19, 2026. With years of GTA 6 post-launch support ahead, Rockstar's next original release could easily land past 2030.
- **Ignore fake trailers and store listings:** Any L.A. Noire 2 teaser trailers, concept leaks, or pre-order pages circulating on social media right now are fake, as neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has announced the game.
- **Replay the original without overpaying:** If the rumor has you itching for noir detective work, remember the original L.A. Noire has been included in the GTA+ Games Library since May 2024 alongside affordable remastered editions on modern consoles and PC.
