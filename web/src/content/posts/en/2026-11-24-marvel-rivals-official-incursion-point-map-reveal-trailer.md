Incursion Point goes live on Thursday, November 26, 2026 at 09:00 UTC as the dedicated 18v18 battlefield for Marvel Rivals' new Chrono-Annihilation mode, and it is strictly outside standard 6v6 Quick Match and Competitive queues. Revealed today as part of Season 11: Doomsday, the map smashes present-day New York straight into 2099's Nueva York, packing in iconic comic landmarks, upward-floating destruction, and massive 36-player teamfights.

## Map Layout & Confirmed Easter Eggs

The battlefield splits down the middle between two clashing timelines. Here are the key landmarks and hidden references spotted in the reveal:

- **Present-Day New York (Xavier's School):** One half features the red-brick towers and grounds of Xavier's School. Look right at the front entrance for a statue of Professor X in his wheelchair, and step inside to find a portrait of Charles Xavier hanging on the wall. Up on the skyline, you can also spot Avengers Tower.
- **Nueva York 2099 (Alchemax & Doom):** The opposing half flips to a sleek white-and-gold Alchemax metropolis anchored by a brightly lit hall housing a massive Doctor Doom statue.
- **Reverse-Gravity Destruction:** Across both halves, buildings fracture with glowing gold and purple incursion cracks—instead of crumbling downward, broken debris floats upward toward the sky.
- **Antimatter Cannon Energy Supply System:** A pillar display breaks down the cannon's three lore fuel sources: Primordial Chronovium, Pure Blood Chronovium, and the Chi of Shou-Lao.
- **Krakoa Seedlings Screen:** Inside a room featuring a central holographic globe, a large monitor flashes the three faced Krakoa Seedlings.

## How to Survive 18v18 on Day One

Jumping from 6v6 into a 36-player lobby completely changes pacing and visual clutter. Use these habits in your first sessions:

- **Map out regroup routes before ego-peeking:** Don't sprint solo down unfamiliar flank lanes on match one. Track where the Anti-Matter Cannons pull both teams and note the safest regroup paths when a push collapses.
- **Anchor one skirmish instead of chasing kills:** Multiple fights erupt simultaneously across 18v18 maps. Pick a lane or objective pocket, help hold captured space, and stop chasing low-HP stragglers away from your group.
- **Abuse high-mobility Strategists like Nightcrawler:** Season 11's Nightcrawler thrives in messy multi-player brawls—he can blink out of focus fire, heal allies while damaging enemies with Brimstone Bombs, and yank teammates out of bad spots (or straight into dives) using Brimstone Dimension.
- **Read the in-game objective card first:** NetEase has confirmed both teams fight around charged Anti-Matter Cannons, but the exact scoring and victory triggers are still unconfirmed ahead of patch day—read the rules panel before locking your comp.

## Pro Tips & Missable Nov 26 Warnings

- **Don't queue Ranked looking for the new map:** Standard 6v6 Quick Match and Competitive won't roll Incursion Point, so head straight into the Chrono-Annihilation tile.
- **Watch the expiration timer on Anniversary Units:** November 26 also unlocks Week 2 of Rivals' Day—opening Days 6–9 of the Times Square Galactic Center check-in, the Ghostworks Arcade (400 Units), and the Van Dyne's event for the free Legendary Deadpool costume. Heads up: 1,500 of the 1,900 login Units expire on December 11 at 09:00 UTC, so burn them in the shop before they vanish.
