## Quick Answer / TL;DR

Wuthering Waves Version 4.0, Embers of the Everbright, officially launches on December 22, 2026 (UTC+8) as the game's first year-long Chapter of the Year set in the Western-inspired New Federation. The 3:39 Official Gameplay Reveal via IGN showcases live test-build combat for upcoming Resonators Anna and Lucielle, a massive mechanized boss encounter, and the brand-new Strider Mech Suit featuring aerial flight, direct combat attacks, and an Underwater Mode. Official attributes, weapons, banner phases, and rerun schedules are still TBA until the Version 4.0 Special Broadcast.

## Key Gameplay Reveal Highlights & How to Prepare for 4.0

Here is everything confirmed in the official reveal and the exact checklist you should follow before Version 3.8 wraps up:

- **Strider Mech Suit Traversal & Combat**: The headline mechanic of the footage is the pilotable Strider Mech Suit, built for high-mobility flight across New Federation terrain, direct combat sequences, and a dedicated Underwater Mode pointing to full underwater exploration. Kuro Games has not yet confirmed whether the suit works outside the New Federation.
- **Anna & Lucielle Live Combat Preview**: Both Resonators appear in action following their individual showcases, fighting across frontier landscapes and taking on a large mechanized boss. Footage is from a test build, so animations and tuning may change before release.
- **Western-Inspired New Federation**: Previewed alongside the 2D animated trailer Beneath Promeus's Embrace, the new region blends arid desert frontiers with a dense, heavily surveilled metropolis.
- **Step 1 – Complete 4.0 Pre-Registration**: Sign up on Kuro Games' official New Federation pre-registration event page right away to lock in free post-launch Astrite rewards.
- **Step 2 – Clear Version 3.8 Content**: Finish the Version 3.8 Lilies in a Dying Dream storyline and active limited-time events like Lahai-Roi Blocks: Turnabout before December 22, since 4.0 launches immediately after 3.8 ends.
- **Step 3 – Hold Your Astrites Until Broadcast**: Save Radiant Tides and Astrites until Kuro Games confirms final kits, roles, and banner phases during the 4.0 Special Broadcast.
