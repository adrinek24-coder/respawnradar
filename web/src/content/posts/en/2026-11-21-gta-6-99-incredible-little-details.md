## Actionable Micro-Mechanics: How to Use GTA 6's Best Hidden Features

- **Unlock on-foot radio and podcasts early:** You cannot play music outside vehicles by default. Head to an electronics store (headphone icon on the map); the closest one to Key Lento is **Zeke's Gadgets** on Cicada Avenue in Hamlet, about 1.5 miles north and just south of Ammu-Nation. Buy Clef Earbuds ($119) or over-the-ear headphones (~$200), open the Weapon Wheel (L1/LB), switch to the Item Wheel (R1/RB), highlight the top-right headphones icon, press Cross/A to equip them, and hold Left on the D-Pad on foot to browse stations or Down for On Demand podcasts.
- **Spot enterable buildings and secret back rooms:** On the world map, **darker blue** structures have enterable interiors while **lighter blue** ones are closed facades. Once inside a store, approach back doors while watching your minimap to see if a hidden back room or staircase exists.
- **Protect your cash before banking unlocks:** You will not get a BuckMe bank account until completing the **Buck Me** mission. Until then, interact with the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom to stash physical cash so getting wasted or busted does not drain your wallet.
- **Trigger free safehouse buffs:** Visit Jason's pet snake **Dolores** at home and feed her a mouse for an early Focus Mode boost. Sleeping, showering, grabbing a coffee from the fridge, and eating snacks stack lasting buffs across health, stamina, and focus.
- **Master hidden vehicle controls and work gear:** Tap Left or Right on the D-Pad inside a car to use working turn signals. You can physically launch boats off trailers, recover them, and tow them across Leonida, plus drive scissor lifts, mobility scooters, and upgraded forklifts strong enough to lift cars.

## Standout Secrets & Spoiler-Free Easter Eggs to Check Out

- **Bonus Prologue escape dialogue:** Most players get caught by the VCPD in the Prologue, but managing an escape unlocks an extra gameplay segment where the prison counselor comments on your getaway and can even name-check your vehicle.
- **Listen to the parrot for hidden loot:** One of your earliest gang hideouts in Jason's hometown is an illegal animal smuggling front; pay attention to what the parrot says for a clue leading to extra loot.
- **Fast & Furious nod on Vespucci Island:** Explore the largest house on Vespucci Island to spot a familiar custom-painted green sports car rocking the license plate **HAD YOU**.
- **Classic Vice City callbacks:** Phil Cassidy returns as the owner of Phil's Ammu-Nation with an unforgettable TV commercial, while the live band at **The Rusty Anchor** bar plays unique covers not heard on the radio—including tracks straight from the original GTA: Vice City.
- **Hamlet tower secret:** Climb all the way to the top of the tall tower just outside Hamlet to uncover a hidden Easter egg.
- **Insane environmental physics:** Characters automatically rest sunglasses on top of their heads when entering home and drop them back down outside, windshield wipers leave rain streaks across scuffed glass, thrown knives stick into walls and can knock doors open on impact, and working clocks match exact in-game time.

## Pro Tips & What to Watch Out For in Leonida

- **Respect your RydeMe drivers:** RydeMe lets you skip rides for an extra $100 as an on-demand fast travel workaround, but drivers hate when you leave the door open and will get you temporarily banned from the app if you cross them.
- **Watch where you park at Jason's house:** Parking too close to the basketball hoop outside Jason's first home risks having a stray shot smash your car windows.
- **Respect wildlife and persistent crime scenes:** Dogs will attack to defend their owners if you insult pedestrians, and crocodiles ignore unconscious bodies until they wake up. Blood stains also persist for multiple in-game days even after sleeping and returning.
- **Adapt to smarter police memory:** Officers track vehicle models, plates, and clothing from witness reports while crime scenes stay hot. Swap getaway cars and toggle hats or glasses from your Item Wheel to reduce recognition faster.
