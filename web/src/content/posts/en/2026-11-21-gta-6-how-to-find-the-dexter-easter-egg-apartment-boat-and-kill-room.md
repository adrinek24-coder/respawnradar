## Quick Answer / TL;DR

You can track down all four confirmed Dexter Easter eggs in GTA 6 as soon as free roam opens after the Prologue. Dexter's condo (**Trinity Apartments**), his **rooftop kill kit**, and his **Slice of Life** lookalike boat (**Lure Predator L35P**) are all clustered on **Washington Island** in Vice Beach (Vice-Dale County). The separate **plastic-wrapped Kill Room** is hidden inside a beach house garage on **Surfside Drive** at the southwest end of **Key Lento** near the Crastenburg Hotel.

## Step-by-Step: How to Find Every Dexter Easter Egg

- **1. Find Dexter's Apartment (Trinity Apartments):** Open your map and look east of Vice City into **Vice-Dale County**, northeast of Penmarch Isle. Zoom into **Vice Beach** and locate **Washington Island**. Look for a pale blue, two-story apartment building sitting directly between the **Pay 'N' Spray** and **Vice Beach Top Fun Drones** icons, featuring a courtyard pool on the north side and a distinctive cylindrical spiral staircase on the south side. The complex is named **Trinity Apartments**—a nod to Season 4's Trinity Killer. Climb the spiral stairs to the second floor and walk west down the exterior walkway to the very last corner unit on the left. Right outside sits the conspicuous AC unit matching Dexter's blood-slide hiding spot from the TV show.
- **2. Loot the Rooftop Kill Kit:** From the covered second-floor corridor (near a ventilation unit on the south side of the complex), find the fixed metal ladder mounted against the wall and climb onto the roof. Walk toward the white dome above the spiral staircase. Spread out on the roof are a syringe (referencing Dexter's M99 tranquilizer), folding knife, rope, handcuffs, blue duct tape, gloves, plastic sheeting, and toolboxes. You can **pick up the knife** and loot **$68 in cash** from the orange toolbox.
- **3. Steal the Slice of Life Lookalike Boat:** Drop down to the waterfront dock on the west side of Trinity Apartments. The first boat moored in the row is a **Lure Predator L35P**. Although Rockstar did not put the name Slice of Life on the hull, the fishing boat closely matches Dexter's iconic vessel and can be stolen and driven right away.
- **4. Break Into Dexter's Kill Room in Key Lento:** For the final confirmed spot, travel to the southwestern stretch of **Key Lento**, much closer to Jason's starting area. Drive along **Surfside Drive** just below the **Crastenburg Hotel** and look for a beach house garage on the beach side of the road. Approach the garage door and trigger the **Break in** prompt. Inside is a full kill room wrapped floor-to-ceiling in plastic sheeting, complete with a central surgical table lined with knives, cleavers, a scalpel, hammers, syringes, and bloody garbage bags.

## Pro Tips & What to Watch Out For

- **Fast Early-Game Trip:** You do not need to progress deep into the campaign to see this. Once you finish the Prologue and take control of Jason in Mariana County, Vice-Dale County is just one county northeast—roughly a five-to-six-minute drive along the freeway.
- **Don't Waste Time Trying to Enter the Condo:** Dexter's corner unit on the second floor is completely sealed with no interior. Focus on the rooftop ladder and the west dock for actual interactive rewards.
- **Watch Out for Unconfirmed Ocean Rumors:** Players have speculated and posted unverified claims about a Bay Harbor Butcher mass grave of sunken body bags off the Vice City coast. Because exact underwater coordinates have not been verified by major guides yet, hold off on spending hours scuba diving until coordinates are confirmed.
- **Unofficial Tribute Approved by Dexter Himself:** Rockstar Games never explicitly mentions Dexter Morgan by name and has not officially confirmed the Easter eggs. Even so, actor Michael C. Hall joked in a Barstool Movies interview that he was informed about the tributes and thought it was pretty cool to see the show folded into the zeitgeist.
