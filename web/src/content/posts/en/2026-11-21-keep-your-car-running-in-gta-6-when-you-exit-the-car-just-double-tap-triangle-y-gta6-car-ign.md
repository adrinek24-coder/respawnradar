**Quick Answer / TL;DR:** To keep your car engine, headlights, hazard lights, and radio running when you step out in GTA 6, simply **double-tap Triangle (PS5) or double-tap Y (Xbox Series X|S)** instead of pressing it once. A standard single tap turns everything off as soon as you exit.

## Step-by-Step: How to Leave Your Engine Running in GTA 6

GTA 6 packs tons of realistic vehicle controls under the hood, and leaving your ride idling is an intentional feature that the game doesn't always spell out clearly. Here is how to do it reliably:

- **Come to a full stop:** Bring your car to a complete halt first so you don't accidentally bail out while moving.
- **Set your lights and radio:** Toggle your headlights or high beams with Circle (PS5) / B (Xbox), pick your radio station, or turn on hazard lights via the Vehicle Controls menu if needed.
- **Double-tap the exit button:** Quickly press **Triangle twice on PS5** or **Y twice on Xbox Series X|S**.
- **Step out with everything running:** Your character exits the car while the engine idles, the headlights or hazards stay lit, and your radio continues playing outside.
- **Make an instant getaway:** When you hop back in, you skip the ignition delay and can immediately hit the gas with R2 (PS5) or RT (Xbox).

## Pro Tips & What to Watch Out For

- **Prep faster getaways for robberies:** Before kicking off a holdup or shootout, park facing your escape route and double-tap exit. Skipping the ignition startup saves crucial seconds when law enforcement closes in.
- **Use headlights as a night spotlight:** Exploring dark backroads or swamps in Leonida at night is much easier when you aim your car at the area and double-tap exit to leave the high beams shining.
- **Cruising on boats:** Don't forget that the exact same double-tap input works at the helm of boats, letting you roam the deck while your vessel stays underway.
- **Watch for radio quirks:** Some players have noticed that while the radio stays on outside the car, re-entering the driver's seat can occasionally turn the radio off, requiring a quick double-tap of D-Pad Left to bring it back.
- **Console-only for now:** Since GTA 6 is currently out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S without a launched PC version yet, official keyboard and mouse bindings for this mechanic are not confirmed.
