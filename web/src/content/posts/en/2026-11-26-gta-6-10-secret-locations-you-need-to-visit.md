Skip aimless roaming across Leonida: these 10 secret GTA 6 locations hand out permanent Max Health and Max Stamina upgrades, a free Chapter 1 Hauners H-AT Rocket Launcher, the Crab Shack VF5 SMG, and wild hidden boss fights. Write down the key codes right now: **CLEAVES**, **0072**, and **0212** for Pelican Valley's Crab Shack, **955214** for the Haunted Marbelle Hotel, and **69185** for the Mount Kalaga watchtower shed.

## How to Find & Clear All 10 Secret Locations

- **1. Pelican Valley Crab Shack (Grassrivers Swamps):** Take a boat to the mangrove compound in southwest Pelican Valley. Flip the breaker switch on the outside wall, inspect the floorboards in the side room, and log into the PC using **CLEAVES**. Back out of the computer and fight off the scripted giant mutant crab ambush. Crack the safe with **0072** (double Specimen 0036) for ~$1,000+ cash, then punch **0212** into the boathouse keypad on the dock for the unique **Crab Shack VF5 SMG** and an $8,000–$10,000 stash bag.
- **2. Circles of Hell House & Hell Arena (Northern Port Gellhorn):** Starting in Chapter 2, go **alone** between midnight and 4 AM (12 AM–2 AM is most reliable) to the corner house on Gellhorn Ave with 'Welcome to the Hell hole' painted outside. Hang out with metalheads Beelzebub and Dave in the red-lit open garage, ride their van to the woods bonfire, and hold **L2 / LT** to stare into the flames. In the DOOM-style Hell arena, kill the exploding demons to turn Satan's black health bar **red**, then unload on him. Rewards: **Hellspawn Comshot 12** shotgun, **permanent Max Health boost**, Circles of Hell shirt, and new tattoos.
- **3. Corsair Key Restroom & Cloning Lab ('The Final Flush'):** Visit the lone public restroom block at the north end of Corsair Key (northeast of Key Lento). Scan the 'Don't Flush' QR poster inside the men's stall with the **WAiNK** app, text back **TRUTH** (saving code **00-00-02**), and meet Dr. Merle at Frank's Waffles in Tequesta at night. At the Hamlet sewage plant, turn all three left panel lights green, pull the lever, flush Dr. Merle, and step into the chamber yourself for a **permanent Max Stamina boost**.
- **4. Haunted Marbelle Hotel (Southern Crosstown, Vice City):** Located on NW 8th Street near Nine1Nine nightclub, one block east of Crosstown Pawn & Gun. Slip in through the unlocked **back door** down the left alley and head straight to the top floor first. Walk up to the corner mannequin to trigger the self-opening stairwell door, blackout whispers, and Silent Hill-style city fog, grab the Old Note on the roof, then enter **955214** on the ground-floor office safe. Loot: thousands in cash, the **Crowex Ambassador 'Breakfast' watch**, and *The Truth Is Out There* trophy/achievement.
- **5. Balsero Underground RV Bunker (West of Vice City):** Reachable right in Chapter 1 (try after *Building a Life* if the hatch stays locked). Cross the road southwest of Griffin Shooting Range into the fenced junkyard, step inside the blue RV loaded with antennas and satellite dishes, and open the hatch in front of the kitchen stove. Grab the free **Hauners H-AT Rocket Launcher** (worth $16,000 at Expanded Ammu-Nation) plus the **Conspiracist Type 9 SMG**.
- **6. Belville 'Simulation' House (Leonard County):** Track down the pale yellow bungalow with two palm trees on Grace Ave in Belville — the exact house Rockstar teased back in the 2021 *GTA Trilogy* remaster. Crouch at the double garage door and force it open to find a self-aware NPC's 'IT'S A SIMULATION' corkboard calling out disappearing cars, doppelgangers, no kids, and 'J + L' being the only free people. Grab *Foiled* Issue 329 on the bed.
- **7. Port Gellhorn Serial Killer Warehouse ('Gellhorn's Missing Tourists'):** Check the Missing Persons posters in Starlet Motel's lobby in Chapter 2 or go straight to the rusty warehouse opposite Moses Watkins' shop (next to Burnout Scooters). Follow the coyotes to the back-right crawlspace, inspect the three trophy lockers, investigate all three victim spots (Pay 'n' Spray junkyard trunk, green trailer floorboards, submerged car north of Wild Hog Point), and take down tip-line host Alvan West.
- **8. Radioactive Crocodile Pond (Grassrivers):** Head directly south of Cottonmouth Lake and southwest of the Act 1 visitor center to find a small pond dumped with glowing radioactive barrels. Standing too close to the drums drains your health, and visibly mutated crocodiles patrol the water.
- **9. Mount Kalaga Firewatch Tower & Locked Shed (Lummox County):** Climb the firewatch tower in Mount Kalaga National Park and check the cabin board for **69185**. Enter **69185** on the wooden shed keypad at the base to inspect a creepy logbook with a mysterious figure sketched on page 2. While you're up there, check the eastern slope below Table Rock for a smoking purple meteorite crater.
- **10. The Vault Secret Radio Station (Downtown Vice City):** Walk into **The Vault** nightclub in Downtown Vice City (south of Jack of Hearts, west of Nine1Nine). Look near the entrance for a red gas-mask poster featuring The Vault logo and scan it with the **WAiNK** phone app (**R1** on PS5 / **RB** on Xbox) to permanently add **The Vault** to your radio wheel.

## Pro Tips & Easy-to-Miss Warnings

- **Don't crack the Marbelle Hotel safe first:** Entering **955214** immediately skips the Silent Hill stairwell blackout and city-wide fog sequence. Always climb upstairs first, and grab the Purple Ratface comic card from the cabinet opposite the ground-floor stairs before you leave.
- **Save outside Circles of Hell:** Drop a manual save as soon as you see the open red garage at night. If you die inside Hell, do **not** leave the campfire area or you forfeit all rewards — walk to the wooden throne and pick 'Eat' to jump right back in.
- **Ignore the intercom at the Hamlet sewage plant:** After flushing Dr. Merle, security tells you not to step inside the chamber — ignore the warning and step in anyway, or you'll walk away without the permanent stamina upgrade.
- **Watch for cops & fence your bag:** Forcing the Belville garage door (or popping the car trunk outside, which hides a snake jumpscare) can alert cops if anyone sees you. To cash in the marked-bills duffel bag from Pelican Valley, sell it at Brian's lockup using door code **7700**.
